Clinical Study Report
Phase III Cardiovascular Safety Study of XYZ-123

Study Title: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Phase III Study to Evaluate the Cardiovascular Safety of XYZ-123 in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

Sponsor: ABC Pharmaceuticals
Protocol Number: XYZ-123-CV-001
FDA IND Number: 123456

Study Objectives:
Primary Objective: To evaluate the cardiovascular safety of XYZ-123 compared to placebo in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Secondary Objectives:
- To assess the efficacy of XYZ-123 on glycemic control
- To evaluate the safety profile of XYZ-123
- To characterize the pharmacokinetics of XYZ-123

Study Design:
This was a Phase III, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter study conducted in accordance with ICH Good Clinical Practice guidelines and FDA regulations. The study was conducted at 150 sites across the United States and Europe.

Study Population:
Adult patients (≥18 years) with type 2 diabetes mellitus and established cardiovascular risk factors were enrolled. A total of 8,000 patients were randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either XYZ-123 10mg once daily or matching placebo.

Primary Endpoint:
The primary endpoint was a composite of cardiovascular death, non-fatal myocardial infarction, or non-fatal stroke, analyzed using a time-to-first-event approach.

Statistical Analysis:
The primary analysis was conducted according to the pre-specified Statistical Analysis Plan using a Cox proportional hazards model. The study was designed to demonstrate non-inferiority of XYZ-123 to placebo with a hazard ratio upper bound of 1.3.

Results:
The primary composite cardiovascular endpoint occurred in 7.4% of patients in the XYZ-123 group and 7.6% of patients in the placebo group (hazard ratio 0.98, 95% CI: 0.89-1.08), demonstrating non-inferiority.

Safety Results:
Adverse events were consistent with the known safety profile of the drug class. There were no new safety signals identified.

Conclusion:
XYZ-123 demonstrated cardiovascular safety in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus, meeting the primary non-inferiority endpoint.

This study supports the regulatory submission for XYZ-123 and will be included in Module 5.3.5.1 of the Common Technical Document as part of the FDA New Drug Application and EMA Marketing Authorization Application.
