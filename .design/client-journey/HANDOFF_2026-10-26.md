**The founder's direction, clarified in the 2026-10-26 continuation.**
- Move specialized former "apps" into core project workflow screens. Standalone app identities are reserved for
  standardized central services; the founder named Projects, Submission Center, Tasking, Vault, CMC, RBM, and Study
  and Protocol Design as examples. This is the durable placement rule, not permission to create another service,
  store, editor, rail entry or commercial grant.
- The product is a set of core places: Projects, Vault, Tasks, Communication, Quality, Reporting, CMC / Module 3,
  Submission Center. Existing homes remain; the latest examples do not retire Communication or Quality. RBM and
  other unfinished placements retain the readiness and admission work in the map.
- Inside a project there are six steps: Plan, Evidence, Author, Review, Submit and Respond.
- Every specialized former "app" becomes a feature inside a place, a step or a document build. Nothing may be lost.
- Tailor those screens by recorded product type, pathway/stage and jurisdiction, then the person's role, assigned
  work and accessible project/study/document/sequence records. Placement does not replace access, entitlement,
  provenance, review/signature or dispatch checks; missing facts remain unknown.

**Recommendations, not additional founder approvals:** the continuation recommended shared homes for Authoring
with Review/Approval, Reporting & analytics, and organisation administration/access/audit. Their implementations
already exist. Use their current owners; this recommendation adds no standalone app or navigation admission.

records the accepted boundary, existing owner paths and current limits. Do not start a parallel architecture plan.
