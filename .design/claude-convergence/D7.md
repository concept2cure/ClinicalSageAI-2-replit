### A.3 Claude's pattern

A submission is worked like a project's document: you see where it stands in one sentence, choose the sequence, and
move through its stages. The one place a verdict is the whole answer — "is this sequence cleared to dispatch?" — is
set as Claude sets an answer: a serif sentence, then its reasons.

```
← [Demo · Biotech] C2C-101 anti-IL-23p19 mAb — plaque psoriasis (IND)
Submission Center                                   [Forms and letters]  [Compile & export]  [New submission]
IND · FDA (US), planning. 1 sequence: 0000, assembling.
(✻ Where does this submission stand?) (✻ What blocks dispatch?) (✻ Plan the next sequence)
Submission [C2C-101 … · IND · FDA (US) ▾]    Sequence [0000 · original · Assembling ▾]     Show the organization's
Submissions  Planner  Builder  Required deliverables  Sequences  Validation  Shadow review  Dispatch
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Dispatch tab:
Cleared to dispatch?
Not yet. Four things stand between sequence 0000 and dispatch.            (Lora 20, the one verdict)
  · 8 validation errors must be resolved.                                 [Open Validation]
  · 6 leaves point at documents not yet approved.                         [Open Builder]
  · No Shadow Review has run.                                             [Run Shadow Review]
  · A Part 11 release signature is required, from someone other than its creator.
8 validation errors · 0 unacknowledged shadow criticals · 0 shadow reviews · 6 leaves · assembling
▸ How the dispatch gate works
Test package                                                       [Assemble a test package]
Package and transmit                                               …
```

- `.page-inner[data-work-area="submission-center"]` (`.sp` metrics; it is its own scroll box). Tabs one row.
- The context row is one line of two labelled `select.c2c-input` (width auto) and a ghost toggle; at ≤640 it wraps.
- Compile & export is the same pattern with a back link "← Submission Center" and the sequence in its sub.
