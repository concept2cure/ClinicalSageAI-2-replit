### 1. Slice 1 — the shared look

**What the screens are today.** All twelve device screens render inside `.mdx-shell` and take their chrome from
`client/src/concept2cure/mdx/app.css`. That sheet has **43 rules that set uppercase**. A probe of the running app
(each of the twelve screens as it opens on the demo 510(k) project) found which of them a person meets on arrival:

| Element | Rule | Today | Seen on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eyebrow above the title | `.page-eyebrow` | 10 px, weight 600, uppercase, letter-spaced: "WORKSTREAM · CLASS II · 510K · …" | 6 screens |
| Stat tile's label | `.metric-label` | 10 px, weight 600, uppercase, letter-spaced | 6 screens |
| Stat tile | `.metric-card[data-tone]` | filled red, amber or green by its tone | 6 screens |
| Table headings | `.tbl thead th` | 10 px, uppercase, letter-spaced | 3 screens |
| Table headings | `.ctable-head` | 10 px, weight 600, uppercase, letter-spaced | 1 screen |
| A blocker's kind | `.eng-blocker-kind` | uppercase over a code the markup writes as "dhf" | 1 screen |

The other 38 uppercase rules style nothing these screens show on arrival: they belong to drawers, detail panes, the
device Vault panels and the Pre-Sub detail, or to no markup at all. In every rule seen, the markup's words are
already written in sentence case ("Workstream", "IRB approved", "K-number"), except the blocker's kind.

**§2's pattern.** Sentence case everywhere. No uppercase coloured eyebrow labels. Status colour only for meaning.
The canonical table heading is `.c2c-v2 .reg-tbl th`: the caption size, weight 500, no letter-spacing, no transform.
The canonical toned figure is `.reg-kpi[data-tone] .reg-kpi-v`: the colour is on the figure.

**The change, in `client/src/concept2cure/mdx/app.css` only:**
1. The four rules a screen shows on arrival (`.page-eyebrow`, `.metric-label`, `.tbl thead th`, `.ctable-head`) set
   no uppercase and no letter-spacing, and take the caption size (`var(--fs-caption)`, 12 px). The eyebrow and the
   tile's label drop to weight 500 and the quiet text colour.
2. A stat tile is not filled by its tone. Its hairline border stays. The tone is carried by the figure:
   `.metric-card[data-tone="err"] .metric-val` is the error colour and `[data-tone="warn"]` the warning colour; `ok`
   needs no colour. Both colours clear AA as text on the page's backgrounds, in light and in dark
   (`design-system/colors_and_type.css`).
3. `.eng-blocker-kind` keeps its uppercase: without it the screen would read "dhf". Its markup is in
   `EngineeringSurface.tsx`, which another session holds; it changes when the markup says "DHF".
4. The other 38 rules are not changed in this slice. Each is changed in the slice that opens its state and reads its
   words, so no label is lowered into a stored value. The sheet's count of uppercase rules only goes down (39 after
   this slice).
5. `client/src/concept2cure/mdx/surface-text-ramp.css` is generated from this sheet; `ci:surface-text-ramp` says
   whether it needs regenerating (it did not).

**Capabilities kept (the ledger for this slice).** Slice 1 removes nothing and moves nothing.

### 2. Slice 2 — the header's layout: it wraps, and works at phone width

**What the screens are today.** Six device screens share one header (`.page-header`): Clinical studies, Software
lifecycle, Device engineering, UDI and labeling, Post-market vigilance and the Pre-Sub manager. It is one flex row
that never wraps, with its actions at the bottom right. Above it the host draws a context line
(`nav.page-actions` in `MdxSurfaceHost.tsx`): the way back to the project, the project's name and, on some screens,
a scope note. That is one row too. Measured in the running app at 390×844 (`probe-header.cjs`,
`header-before-390.json`):

| Screen | Text block | Title | Context line | First figure at | An action cut off |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clinical studies | 161 px | 2 lines | 63 px | 383 px | — |
| Software lifecycle | 161 px | 2 lines | 63 px | 383 px | — |
| Device engineering | 161 px | 2 lines | 63 px | 419 px | — |
| UDI and labeling | 161 px | 2 lines | 63 px | 363 px | — |
| Post-market vigilance | 135 px | 3 lines | 336 px, three columns | 965 px, below the fold | — |
| Pre-Sub manager | 113 px | 2 lines | 63 px | 500 px | "Ask AnA to start a Q-Sub", 284–472 px of 390 |

The Pre-Sub manager's one primary action could not be read or pressed in full on a phone.

**§2's pattern.** The canonical page header (`.c2c-v2 .ph`, `v2/styles/app-v2.css`): the text block and the actions
wrap; the text block keeps a floor of `min(420px, 100%)`; the actions are level with the top of the text; at 640 px
and under every part after the text block takes a full line and the title is 22 px. Labeling and Human factors,
which are already on it, read well at 390 px.

**The change, in `client/src/concept2cure/mdx/app.css` only:**
1. `.page-header` wraps, with `align-items: flex-start` and the canonical `gap: 12px 24px`. Its padding and its
   hairline stay: the device page's blocks each carry their own inset.
2. The header's text block takes the canonical floor: `flex: 1 1 0%; min-width: min(420px, 100%)`.
3. `.page-title` may break inside a long word (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`), as the canonical title does.
4. At 640 px and under: every part after the text block takes a full line (`flex: 1 1 100%`), the title is 22 px,
   and a row of actions (`.page-actions`) wraps. The host's context line is such a row, so it wraps too. Its markup,
   in a file another session holds, is not edited.
5. Wider than a phone a row of actions stays on one line, as it was. A first build let it wrap at every width, and
   Post-market vigilance's scope note then took its own line at 1440 and moved the screen down 19 px; that was
   taken back before anything was committed.

**Capabilities kept.** No markup, text, control or behaviour changes. Every word and every action of the header and
of the context line is still drawn, in the same order.

**Acceptance checks.**

  passes after (5 of 5). The two cases that pass both before and after pin that a row of actions does not wrap above
  phone width, and that none of the header's rules is hidden.
- At 390×844, on all six screens: the title is on one line, the text block is the full 278 px, no action is cut

- At 1440×900, on all six: the title, the header's height, the context line's height and the first figure are where

### 2b. Slice 2b — a pathway page does not scroll sideways on a phone

**What the screens are today.** The 510(k), PMA and Diagnostics screens share the pathway layouts. At 390×844 the
device page's own container (`.mdx-shell .page`) scrolled to 710, 712 and 720 px (`probe-wide.cjs`). The document
itself did not scroll, so a walk that reads the document's width reports nothing. Five layouts ran past the edge:

| Layout | Rule today | At 390 px |
|---|---|---|
| The pathway tab row | `.pwt-bar`: a flex row with no scroll of its own | its later tabs reach 710 px |
| The stage strip, and PMA's phases | `.stage-strip`, `.phases`: grids of seven and ten equal columns | reach 504 and 712 px |
| The two-column body | `.col2`: `1.4fr 1fr` at every width | the second column reaches 707 px |
| The health cards | `.health`: four columns at every width | the fourth card reaches 483 px |
| The filing panel's controls | a row of two selects and a button in a `.section-hdr` that does not wrap | reaches 574 px |

They carried their controls with them: 47 on the 510(k) screen, 6 on each of the other two.

**§2's pattern.** A strip that does not fit scrolls inside itself (`.c2c-v2 .tabs`, `.seg`); columns stack on a
phone; nothing makes a page scroll sideways.

**The change.**
1. `mdx/pathway-tabs.css`: `.pwt-bar` scrolls inside itself (`overflow-x: auto; overflow-y: hidden;
   scrollbar-width: none`, as the canonical tab row). So that the active tab's underline is not clipped, the row's
   hairline is drawn inside its box (`box-shadow: inset 0 -1px 0 var(--border)`) over one pixel of bottom padding,
   in place of a bottom border: the row is the height it was.
2. `mdx/app.css`, at 640 px and under: `.stage-strip` and `.phases` scroll inside themselves; `.col2` is one
   column; `.health` is two columns; `.section-hdr` wraps, its children may shrink and its row of controls wraps; a
   select in it is never wider than the screen.
3. No markup is edited. `pathway/PathwayPanes.tsx` is held by another session; `K510Surface.tsx`, `PmaSurface.tsx`,
   `IvdSurface.tsx` and `EstarFilingPanel.tsx` are not touched.

**Capabilities kept.** Every tab, stage, panel, card and control is still drawn, in the same order. Wider than a
phone each layout is as it was.

**Acceptance checks.**
- `client/src/concept2cure/mdx/__tests__/pathwayPagesFitAPhone.test.ts` fails before the change (6 of 7) and passes
  after. Its seventh case pins the layouts above phone width.
- At 390×844 the four pathway screens' container is 390 px wide and scrolls to 390 px, and the only controls past
  the edge are the later tabs, inside the tab row.
- At 1440×900 the captures before and after differ only where the data did (the audit trail's count), by a
  pixel-by-pixel comparison.
- The four screens before and after at both widths, in light and dark (16 pairs): the same failed requests, no new
  console error, the same headings, the same number of controls, the same length of text.

### 3. Slice 3 — the stat tiles become one quiet line of figures

**What the screens are today.** Six device screens open with a row of stat tiles: Clinical studies (two rows),
Software lifecycle, Device engineering, UDI and labeling, Post-market vigilance and the Pre-Sub manager. Each tile is a
bordered box holding exactly three things, read in this order: a label, a figure in a 28 px serif, and a note (probe
of every tile, `docs/evidence/DESIGN/D8/slice-3/tiles-before-1440.json`). A row is 121–148 px tall at 1440 px. At 390
px the tiles stack one to a row, 370–506 px tall, and the work under them starts at 735–971 px, below the first
screen, on every one of the six.

**§2's pattern.** Stat tiles are retired as permanent chrome. P-122 keeps every figure a screen shows: Quality's
registers show theirs as one quiet line (`.c2c-v2 .reg-kpis`, `.reg-kpi`, `.reg-kpi-v`, `.reg-kpi-l`, app-v2.css):
no box, the figure at the section size and weight 600, the label in the small muted text, a tone on the figure.

**The change.**
1. `mdx/app.css`: `.metrics-row` is a wrapping flex line. `.metric-card` has no border, ground, radius or padding.
   Above phone width it is a two-row grid, `minmax(0, max-content) minmax(min-content, 1fr)`: the label, then the
   figure beside it; the note under both. The figure's column never goes below the figure's width and the label may
   wrap. At 640 px and under each figure is a wrapping line instead (the figure moves under its label rather than out
   of its space), and the gap between figures is 20 px. `.metric-label` takes `--fs-small` and the muted text;
   `.metric-val` the section size, weight 600, sans; `.metric-meta` the caption size and the muted text. A tone still
   colours the figure (slice 1a). `.metrics-compact` is the same line, set closer.
2. **The order on screen is the markup's**: label, figure, note. The canonical register writes the figure first; the
   device markup writes the label first, and reordering it by CSS would make what a person sees differ from what a
   screen reader reads.
3. `mdx/surfaces/SoftwareSurface.tsx`: two word figures ("Not determined", and the documentation level) lose an
   inline 18 px, set when a figure was 28 px, and the level is written with its first letter capital in code instead
   of title-cased by a style ("Not Determined"; §2 is sentence case).
4. `mdx/surface-text-ramp.css` is generated and does not change: a figure's box painted `--bg-000`, not a tinted
   ground.

**Capabilities kept.** Every label, figure, unit, tone and note is still drawn, in the same order, at the top of the
screen where it was. Nothing moves below the fold; the work under the figures moves up.

**Acceptance checks.**
- `client/src/concept2cure/mdx/__tests__/tilesAreALineOfFigures.test.ts` fails before the change (7 of 9) and passes
  after. Its two other cases pin the tone on the figure and that no part of a figure is hidden.
- At 320, 390, 720 and 1440 px, with normal and doubled text, no part of a figure runs out of its space or overlaps
  another (`probe-reflow.cjs`; the accessibility review's WCAG 1.4.4 and 1.4.10 risk).
- `SoftwareSurface.emptyAskAna.test.tsx`: two new cases fail before (the inline style; "basic") and pass after.
- `sharedLookRetiredPatterns.test.ts` (slice 1): the tile label's pinned size moves from the caption to the small
  size, as the canonical register label's; its other pins are unchanged.
- In the running app, at 1440 and 390, light and dark: the same failed requests, headings, controls and text before
  and after on all six screens; no text below AA; each row shorter and the work under it higher.

**After slice 1, still in the earlier design (for slices 2–4):** the eyebrow as a separate line above the title; the
