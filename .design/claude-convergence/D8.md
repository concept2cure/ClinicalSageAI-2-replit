### 1. Slice 1 — the shared look

**What the screens are today.** All twelve device screens render inside `.mdx-shell` and take their chrome from
`client/src/concept2cure/mdx/app.css`. That sheet has **43 rules that set uppercase**. A probe of the running app
(each of the twelve screens as it opens on the demo 510(k) project) found which of them a person meets on arrival:

| Element | Rule | Today | Seen on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eyebrow above the title | `.page-eyebrow` | 10 px, weight 600, uppercase, letter-spaced: "WORKSTREAM · CLASS II · 510K · …" | 6 screens |
| Stat tile's label | `.metric-label` | 10 px, weight 600, uppercase, letter-spaced | 6 screens |
| Stat tile | `.metric-card[data-tone]` | filled red, amber or green by its tone | 6 screens |
| Table headings | `.tbl thead th` | 10 px, uppercase, letter-spaced | 3 screens |
| Table headings | `.ctable-head` | 10 px, weight 600, uppercase, letter-spaced | 1 screen |
| A blocker's kind | `.eng-blocker-kind` | uppercase over a code the markup writes as "dhf" | 1 screen |

The other 38 uppercase rules style nothing these screens show on arrival: they belong to drawers, detail panes, the
device Vault panels and the Pre-Sub detail, or to no markup at all. In every rule seen, the markup's words are
already written in sentence case ("Workstream", "IRB approved", "K-number"), except the blocker's kind.

**§2's pattern.** Sentence case everywhere. No uppercase coloured eyebrow labels. Status colour only for meaning.
The canonical table heading is `.c2c-v2 .reg-tbl th`: the caption size, weight 500, no letter-spacing, no transform.
The canonical toned figure is `.reg-kpi[data-tone] .reg-kpi-v`: the colour is on the figure.

**The change, in `client/src/concept2cure/mdx/app.css` only:**
1. The four rules a screen shows on arrival (`.page-eyebrow`, `.metric-label`, `.tbl thead th`, `.ctable-head`) set
   no uppercase and no letter-spacing, and take the caption size (`var(--fs-caption)`, 12 px). The eyebrow and the
   tile's label drop to weight 500 and the quiet text colour.
2. A stat tile is not filled by its tone. Its hairline border stays. The tone is carried by the figure:
   `.metric-card[data-tone="err"] .metric-val` is the error colour and `[data-tone="warn"]` the warning colour; `ok`
   needs no colour. Both colours clear AA as text on the page's backgrounds, in light and in dark
   (`design-system/colors_and_type.css`).
3. `.eng-blocker-kind` keeps its uppercase: without it the screen would read "dhf". Its markup is in
   `EngineeringSurface.tsx`, which another session holds; it changes when the markup says "DHF".
4. The other 38 rules are not changed in this slice. Each is changed in the slice that opens its state and reads its
   words, so no label is lowered into a stored value. The sheet's count of uppercase rules only goes down (39 after
   this slice).
5. `client/src/concept2cure/mdx/surface-text-ramp.css` is generated from this sheet; `ci:surface-text-ramp` says
   whether it needs regenerating (it did not).
