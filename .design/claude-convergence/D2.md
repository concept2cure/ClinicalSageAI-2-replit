#### 1.3 Claude's pattern

claude.ai Projects: the page title in the serif, one "New project" button, a search field, a sort control, and a
two-column grid of quiet cards. Each card holds the project's name, one or two lines of its description, and
"Updated 3 days ago". Nothing else.

- **Container.** `.pj-index` is the surface's own scroll container (`projects` is registered `full: true` in
  `surfaceViews.ts:527`, so it cannot be `.page-inner`). Its content is centred at **max 1120px** with D0's page
  padding: `32px 40px 96px`; `24px` gutters at ≤1023px; `20px 16px 72px` at ≤640px.
- **Header** (D0 `.ph`): `.ph-title` "Projects" (Lora 26/500, 22 at ≤640). Right: `.ph-acts` with one
  `.btn.primary` "New project" (`I.plus`, 16px). No eyebrow, no sub-line, no second button.
  Header to toolbar: 24px.
- **Toolbar, one row** (`.pj-toolbar`, `display:flex; flex-wrap:wrap; gap:8px; align-items:center`):
  search (flex `1 1 280px`, max 420px, 32px, D0 input chrome with a 16px search icon inside), then the filter selects
  (each a `select.c2c-input`, `width:auto`, 32px): Client (only with more than one client), Workstream, Pathway (only
  when there is a choice), Status (only when there is a choice), then a flexible gap, then the Grid / List `.seg`
  (D0, 28px items). Toolbar to grid: 16px.
- **Grid** (`.pj-cards`): two columns, `gap:16px`; one column at ≤900px. Each card is D0's clickable `.pj-card`
  (radius 12, hairline, padding 16, hover `--bg-050` + `--border-strong`, no shadow), `min-height:120px`, laid out as:
  1. `.pj-card-t`: the title, 15px weight 600 `--text-100`, line-height 1.35, clamped to 2 lines.
  2. `.pj-card-m`: one line of description, 13px `--text-300`, line-height 1.5, clamped to 2 lines, `margin-top:4px`.
  3. `.pj-card-r` (pushed to the bottom with `margin-top:auto; padding-top:12px`): left "LMN-401 · Updated Oct 31";
     right "Due Feb 28, 2027" when a target date is set. 12px `--text-300`, tabular figures.
  4. Only when the status is not active: one line in its tone with its icon, e.g. `I.alertTriangle` "Blocked · No
     cause recorded" (`--warning`), or "Archived" (`--text-300`).
- **List** (`.ctable`): D0 table type (12px sentence-case header on no band, 13px rows, hover `--bg-050`). The list is
