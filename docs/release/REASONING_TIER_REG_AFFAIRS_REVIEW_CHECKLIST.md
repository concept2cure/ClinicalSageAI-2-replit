# Reasoning Tier Regulatory Affairs Review Checklist

**Status:** Active checklist  
**Date:** 2026-03-27

Use this checklist for Regulatory Affairs sign-off in human UAT cycles.

---

## A) Submission-Context Fit

- [ ] Output is aligned to target pathway context (e.g., 510(k), CER, eCTD section intent).
- [ ] Claims remain within intended scope and indication framing.
- [ ] Required caveats/disclaimers are present for non-final analyses.

## B) Claim and Evidence Quality

- [ ] No unsupported critical claims.
- [ ] Contradictory evidence is explicitly surfaced.
- [ ] Benefit-risk framing is proportionate and reviewable.

## C) Medical Writing Controls

- [ ] Red-flag phrase scan completed (`docs/evals/MEDICAL_WRITING_RED_FLAG_PATTERNS.md`).
- [ ] No unresolved high-risk overclaim patterns.
- [ ] Uncertainty language calibrated to evidence quality.

## D) Governance and Traceability

- [ ] `artifact_id`, `artifact_version`, `artifact_status` captured.
- [ ] `provenance_ref` and `audit_ref` resolvable.
- [ ] `downloadable_output_ref` present when downloadable outputs exist.

## E) Decision

- [ ] Regulatory Affairs recommendation recorded (Approve / Conditional / Reject).
- [ ] Required remediation items assigned and dated.
- [ ] Regulatory Affairs sign-off completed.
