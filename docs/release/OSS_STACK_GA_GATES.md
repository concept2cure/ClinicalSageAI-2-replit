# OSS Stack GA Gates

## GA promotion prerequisites
1. Beta burn-in window completed with no critical governance regressions.
2. Regression suite pass on domain-specific golden regulatory tasks.
3. Provenance/citation integrity meets production thresholds.
4. Policy decisioning remains centralized, explainable, and trace-correlated.
5. Rollback drills succeed for each subsystem flag.

## GA-specific decisions

### Byaldi
- Can move beyond pilot only after benchmark superiority and operational stability are proven.

### E2B
- Can expand scope only after repeated proof of isolation, bounded execution, and governed output routing.

### OPA migration
- OPA can become primary policy engine only when decision parity + fail-closed guarantees are proven against current in-app policy baseline.

## GA no-compromise rules
- No hidden review/export bypasses.
- No untraceable policy decisions.
- No unbounded long-running jobs.
- No uncitable evidence responses in regulated workflows.
