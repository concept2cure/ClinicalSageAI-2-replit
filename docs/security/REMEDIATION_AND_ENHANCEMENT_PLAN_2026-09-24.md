## How to use this plan

- **Owner codes.** `eng` = a Claude session on the named lane, under `CLAUDE.md` Rules 0–2 (one lane, evidence
  under `docs/evidence/<row>/`, files another lane touched in the last 24 hours are not raced); `founder` = an
  account, a signature, a purchase or a product decision; `counsel` = a lawyer (US, EU, Japan as marked);
  `vendor` = a third party (pen-test firm, compliance platform, auditor).
- **Acceptance.** Every `eng` item is shown failing first: a red test, gate output or reproduction filed under the
  row's evidence folder, then the same check green. An item without that pair is not done.
- **Stages.** P0 closes what is exploitable or fail-open at head and must precede any production tenant. P1 is what
  makes D6 green and what a procurement team tests. P2 is market entry for EU and Japanese buyers and HIPAA-bearing
  US tenants. P3 is continuous assurance. Within a stage, items are ordered by risk reduced per day of effort.
- **Founder decisions** that gate items are listed in §6; an item whose decision is open is not blocked from its
  engineering half.
- **Progress, 2026-09-25 (this session's tranche).** Closed: P0-1, P0-3, P0-5, P0-6, P0-7, P0-13a, P0-16a.
