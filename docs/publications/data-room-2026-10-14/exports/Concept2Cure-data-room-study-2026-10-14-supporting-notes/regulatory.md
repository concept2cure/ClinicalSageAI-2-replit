# Regulatory research for a life sciences evidence/data platform

Research cut-off: 2026-10-14. Scope: medicines/biologics, medical devices, IVDs/diagnostics, combination products and their development, authorisation, change, registration and post-market workflows. This is a product requirements analysis, not a complete legal inventory. Sources are primary regulators, governments and standards bodies. Undated pages were inspected on the cut-off date; dates below distinguish publication, effective dates and planned milestones. No production connector, filing or customer data changes were performed.

## Core conclusion

**One evidence and product-data core, many versioned filing adapters.** A catalog/data room should model the relationship between a regulated product, intended use/claim, jurisdiction, requirement, source evidence, transformation, author, review, and exact submitted version. A drug eCTD envelope is not a universal global filing format; device/IVD technical documentation, eSTAR, IMDRF tables of contents, local portals, structured product registries and vigilance reports are distinct outputs. Product class, application type, regulator, date and submission lifecycle determine the applicable rules.

The defensible product opportunity is a traceable evidence system that can explain what supports each claim, what is missing, and which filings or commitments a change affects. Current regulator programmes increasingly use structured metadata and product master data, so a PDF-only room leaves substantial value unused. These are architectural recommendations inferred from the sourced requirements below.

## United States: medicines and study data

**eCTD v4.0: accepted, with restricted scope; do not label universally mandatory.** FDA CDER/CBER accept new regulatory applications in v4.0 from **2024-09-16**. FDA identifies forward compatibility for existing v3.2.2 applications and two-way communication as future phases. Its sample process currently assesses new applications only. The parent eCTD page identifies new NDA, BLA, ANDA, IND and master files as supported routes. A platform therefore needs both v3.2.2 and v4.0 lifecycle handling, region-specific Module 1, vocabularies, IDs, validation and acknowledgements; it should not silently migrate an existing dossier.

Rule-package versions/support dates must be pinned, rather than assuming the newest ICH release is automatically supported by FDA.

**Structured study-data support:** FDA describes CDISC SEND for nonclinical tabulations, SDTM for clinical tabulations, ADaM for analyses, and Define-XML metadata. Applicable standard/version dates, study start dates, submission type, exceptions and relevant centre matter. ## United States: devices, IVDs and quality

**eSTAR obligations differ by route.** 510(k) submissions, unless exempted, require eSTAR from **2023-10-01**; De Novo requests from **2025-10-01**. FDA's current page also lists dual 510(k)/CLIA waiver IVD submissions and CDRH/CBER combination-product 510(k)/De Novo submissions in mandatory scope. PMA applications/specified supplements, Q-Submissions, IDEs and 513(g) requests are listed as voluntary routes. Current nIVD/IVD templates are v7.1 on the inspected page; June 1 2026 update says new human-factors content becomes effective **2026-08-01**.

The eSTAR is a conditional dynamic PDF, not an ordinary document or open JSON API. FDA exposes XML data import/export, but attachments must be reattached. Concurrent coauthoring of the dynamic PDF is unsupported. Incorrect branch answers can hide required questions and trigger screening holds. The regulatory route adapter therefore needs a controlled question model, answers tied to source evidence, attachment manifests, version/branch checks and a tested handoff to the official template. It should describe capabilities as preparation/export until official-template execution is verified.

- [FDA eSTAR Program](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/how-study-and-market-your-device/estar-program), page update **2026-06-01**, inspected 2026-10-14.
- [FDA 510(k)](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/premarket-submissions-selecting-and-preparing-correct-submission/premarket-notification-510k), mandatory date 2023-10-01.
- [FDA De Novo](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/premarket-submissions-selecting-and-preparing-correct-submission/de-novo-classification-request), mandatory date 2025-10-01.
- [FDA CDRH Portal](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/industry-medical-devices/send-and-track-medical-device-premarket-submissions-online-cdrh-portal), inspected 2026-10-14. Submission preparation and actual portal transmission are different capabilities.

**Quality-system support:** FDA QMSR is effective **2026-02-02**, incorporating ISO 13485:2016 by reference with FDA-specific provisions. A data catalog can connect design/development records, risk controls, verification, validation, supplier records, CAPA and clinical evidence to the filing record. Avoid assuming an ISO certificate alone establishes FDA compliance. Computer Software Assurance guidance covers device production and quality management software; use it within its stated scope, not as blanket relief for every clinical or regulatory software function.

- [FDA QMSR](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/postmarket-requirements-devices/quality-management-system-regulation-qmsr), inspected 2026-10-14, effective 2026-02-02.
- [FDA Computer Software Assurance for Production and Quality Management System Software](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/computer-software-assurance-production-and-quality-management-system-software), current **February 2026** guidance supersedes September 2025 guidance.

## European Union: medicines

**eCTD v4.0 rollout is phased.** EMA's **2026-07-08** notice confirms optional v4.0 for **new centrally authorised product MAAs since 2025-12-22**, while v3.2.2 remains accepted. Strongly recommended use is planned for **Q1 2027** and mandatory use for **Q1 2028** for new CAP MAAs. Existing-lifecycle forward compatibility is in pilots and readiness assessment. The March 2026 v1.1 validation criteria became applicable **2026-07-15**. Non-CAP new-MAA technical pilot is planned for Q3/Q4 2026; it must not be advertised as completed or universally available based on that plan. Prior contact with EMA is requested for v4.0 CAP submissions.

- [EMA eCTD v4.0 current project notices and specifications](https://esubmission.ema.europa.eu/eCTD%20NMV/eCTD.html), **2026-07-08**, inspected 2026-10-14.
- [EMA eCTD v3.2 regional materials](https://esubmission.ema.europa.eu/ectd/), inspected 2026-10-14.
- [ICH eCTD v4.0](https://ich.org/page/ich-electronic-common-technical-document-ectd-v40), inspected 2026-10-14; latest ICH IG v1.7/CV package v1.0.3 endorsed June 2026. Harmonised publication does not equal each region's production acceptance.

**IDMP/SPOR is a product-data service programme, not a single switch date.** EMA describes phased implementation of substance (SMS), product (PMS), organisation (OMS), and referential (RMS) master data. OMS/RMS feed electronic application forms. EMA states authorised-medicine data submission/maintenance is already mandatory via XEVPRM and will move to IDMP-compatible format in due course. PMS has implementation guides, API/security specifications, migration rules, public/read and write API material, and use cases linked to shortage reporting. Do not state that all IDMP data fields are universally mandatory now, or that PMS replaces all Article 57/XEVMPD flows today.

- [EMA SPOR master data overview](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory-overview/research-development/data-medicines-iso-idmp-standards-overview/substance-product-organisation-referential-spor-master-data), inspected 2026-10-14; onboarding reference updated **2025-11-05**.
- [EMA PLM PMS guidance and training](https://plm-portal.ema.europa.eu/Guidance/article/KA-01032/en-us/), inspected 2026-10-14; FAQ listed as Jan 2026. The page supplies API, IDMP data matrix, migration and process references.

Platform implication: maintain governed product/pack/substance/manufacturer/authorisation entities and stable external IDs. Reconcile document statements against structured master records and show divergence. Adapters should manage mappings and change proposals before any authorised API write.

## European Union: devices and IVDs

MDR/IVDR technical documentation and conformity-assessment evidence differ from a drug CTD. Route-specific support should include intended purpose, risk/classification rationale, GSPR evidence matrix, design/manufacturing, risk management, verification/validation, clinical evaluation for devices and performance evaluation for IVDs, labelling/IFU, PMS/PMCF or PMPF, certificates and declarations. Model diagnostic analyte, specimen, population, setting and decision role explicitly; they change the relevance of performance claims.

IVDR Annex XIII and MDCG guidance distinguish **scientific validity, analytical performance and clinical performance**. This calls for three linked evidence tracks, rather than a generic clinical-document folder. Biomarker-drug/companion-diagnostic links and shared studies should preserve each product's separate claims and route obligations.

- [EU Regulation 2017/745 (MDR)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/745/oj), published 2017-05-05; inspect consolidated version and later amendments for implementation.
- [EU Regulation 2017/746 (IVDR)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/746/oj), published 2017-05-05; inspect consolidated version and later amendments for implementation.
- [MDCG 2022-2 IVD clinical evidence guidance](https://health.ec.europa.eu/latest-updates/mdcg-2022-2-guidance-general-principles-clinical-evidence-vitro-diagnostic-medical-devices-ivds-2022-01-27_en), **2022-01-27**; guidance, not standalone legislation.
- [Commission IVDR overview](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-vitro-diagnostics/overview_en), inspected 2026-10-14; describes risk classes and staggered transitional provisions under amendments. Legacy-device eligibility is conditional; do not use a single transition date for every IVD.

**EUDAMED actual rollout:** the Commission says Actor registration, UDI/Device registration, Notified Bodies & Certificates and Market Surveillance became mandatory **2026-05-28**. The last of these is for competent authorities/Commission. Clinical Investigation & Performance Studies remains under analysis/development and Vigilance/PMS is still in development. Commission Decision 2025/2371 (published **2025-11-27**) triggered the six-month transition. Product/role-specific backfill and transitional rules remain relevant; 'four modules mandatory' is not identical to 'every historical record due that day'.

- [Commission EUDAMED overview](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-eudamed/overview_en), inspected 2026-10-14.
- [Commission EUDAMED getting ready](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-eudamed/getting-ready_en), inspected 2026-10-14.

Platform implication: add Basic UDI-DI/UDI-DI, manufacturer/representative/SRN, certificate and device relationships; validated XML/bulk exports where supported; role-aware registration tasks; immutable submitted snapshots and reconciliation. Do not present EUDAMED as the channel for every clinical evidence package or every vigilance report already.

## Additional lifecycle filings beyond authorisation dossiers

These workflows demonstrate why a single eCTD publisher cannot cover every filing need.

- **EU medicine clinical-trial applications:** CTIS is mandatory for new initial applications from **2023-01-31**; the Commission confirms all ongoing EU/EEA medicinal-product trials must follow CTR through CTIS from **2025-01-31**. Support Part I/Part II content, country/language requirements, amendments, requests for information, transparency/redaction and results workflows. Drug-device/IVD combined studies require assessment of the interface between regimes, rather than assuming one trial approval covers everything. [Commission clinical-trials page](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medicinal-products/clinical-trials_en), inspected 2026-10-14.
- **US drug/biologic safety ICSRs:** FDA's AEMS page (formerly FAERS) confirms **2026-10-01** mandatory E2B(R3) for specified human drug/biologic/led-combination postmarketing ICSRs submitted via ESG NextGen; the April 6 2026 announcement extended the transition by six months. IND-study ICSRs for commercial IND sponsors via database transmission require E2B(R3) from **2026-04-01**. SRP users are distinguished; IND-exempt BA/BE electronic submission is voluntary. The narrative part of a periodic safety report and its individual cases have different submission handling. Product-specific scope and routing matter. [FDA AEMS electronic submissions](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/fda-adverse-event-monitoring-system-aems/fda-adverse-event-monitoring-system-aems-electronic-submissions), inspected 2026-10-14.
- **EU medicine safety:** EudraVigilance has ICH E2B(R3) and EU-specific implementation/business rules, onboarding, test phases, acknowledgements and data-masking requirements. Use a regional case adapter, qualified safety reviewer and deadline controls. [EMA EudraVigilance electronic reporting](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory-overview/research-development/pharmacovigilance-research-development/eudravigilance/eudravigilance-electronic-reporting), inspected 2026-10-14.
- **US device incidents:** FDA says manufacturers/importers must report individual adverse events electronically unless exempt; user facilities have different options, and distributors have recordkeeping obligations. ESG NextGen USP replaced legacy WebTrader **2025-04-14**. [FDA eMDR](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/mandatory-reporting-requirements-manufacturers-importers-and-device-user-facilities/emdr-electronic-medical-device-reporting), update **2026-04-10**. Do not apply drug E2B(R3) rules to every device incident merely because both use structured safety messages.
- **US product/facility data:** FDA requires drug registration/listing submissions in SPL XML. Maintain NDC, product/pack, facility and label relationships with evidence and dates; an SPL listing does not establish marketing approval. [FDA drug registration/listing instructions](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/electronic-drug-registration-and-listing-system-edrls/drug-registration-and-listing-instructions), [FDA SPL resources](https://www.fda.gov/industry/fda-data-standards-advisory-board/structured-product-labeling-resources), inspected 2026-10-14.

## AI governance: distinguish deployed rules, guidance and proposals

1. **FDA/EMA joint good AI practice principles, January 2026:** ten principles were released **2026-01-14**, covering human responsibility, risk, context of use, standards, expertise, data/documentation, model development, performance, lifecycle and communication. They support an evidence-based governance model; they are principles, not proof that a particular AI model or platform is approved. [FDA principles](https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/artificial-intelligence-drug-development/guiding-principles-good-ai-practice-drug-development), [EMA January 2026 joint document](https://www.ema.europa.eu/system/files/documents/other/2026-01-13_ai-guiding-principles_ema-fda_en.pdf).
2. **FDA drug/biologic AI credibility guidance remains DRAFT** on the inspected page, dated **January 2025**. It addresses AI-produced information/data used for safety, effectiveness or quality decisions and a risk-based credibility framework. Treat it as a proposal/current draft recommendation, not a final binding mandate. [FDA draft guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/considerations-use-artificial-intelligence-support-regulatory-decision-making-drug-and-biological).
3. **EMA reflection paper is adopted**, final CHMP adoption **2024-09-09**, spanning medicinal-product lifecycle. [EMA adopted reflection paper](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/scientific-guidelines/use-artificial-intelligence-ai-medicinal-product-lifecycle).
4. **Device PCCP guidance is FINAL**, current FDA version **August 2025**, for AI-enabled device software functions. Device clients need evidence for pre-specified changes, modification protocols and impact assessment where that route applies. This is a client-product filing workflow; it does not automatically mean the regulatory authoring platform is itself a medical device. [FDA PCCP guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/marketing-submission-recommendations-predetermined-change-control-plan-artificial-intelligence).

Architecture implications (recommendations):

- Assistive drafting/extraction: every material statement links to exact evidence version and page/table/cell; show contradictions and absent support; prohibit invented study results or inferred approvals. Label extracted/proposed/reviewed states and preserve translations beside originals.
- AI used to generate regulatory evidence: register context of use, risk, data suitability/representativeness, analytical validation, statistical assessment, performance limits, reproducibility, model changes and sponsor acceptance. A citation alone does not validate a derived result.
- Pin model, prompt, retrieval corpus and tool versions; preserve run manifests and approval. Evaluate on real document classes, numerical extraction, claim consistency, regulatory applicability, multilingual meaning and evidence sufficiency. Use appropriately governed test material.
- Human owners approve regulatory strategy, classification, statistical conclusions, safety/benefit interpretation, attestations, signatures and released packages. Preparation automation can recommend; qualified reviewers determine sufficiency and transmit authorised filings.
- Export deterministic packages with a manifest, checksums, vocabulary/schema versions, conformance findings, resolved/unresolved exceptions, reviewer signoff and proof of source freeze. Independent route validation should run after content preparation.

## Data residency and cross-border policy

Residency and filing format are separate dimensions. A regulator's portal requirement does not by itself establish where all upstream storage or AI inference must run. Apply policy per tenant, project, participant population, data class, controller/processor relationship, consent/legal basis, destination and contract. Region labels alone are insufficient. The same controls must cover raw documents, embeddings, prompts, caches, logs, model training, backups and human support access.

- **EU/EEA:** GDPR international-transfer rules require an applicable Chapter V basis/safeguards. Do not claim GDPR mandates all life sciences data always remain in Europe. Pseudonymisation is not automatically anonymisation. [EDPB Guidelines 05/2021 on territorial scope/international transfers](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/guidelines-052021-on-the-interplay-between-the-application-of-article-3-and-the_en), final-version landing page **2023-02-24**; [EDPB clinical-trials/GDPR opinion](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/legislative-opinion/opinion-32019-concerning-the-questions-and-answers-on-the-interplay_en), **2019-01-23**.
- **US HIPAA:** HHS allows overseas cloud storage/processing subject to applicable protections, risk analysis and business-associate arrangements; geography can affect risk. HIPAA does not cover all sponsor data merely because it is health-related. [HHS cloud guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/health-information-technology/cloud-computing/index.html); [HHS overseas cloud FAQ](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/faq/do-the-hipaa-rules-allow-a-covered-entity-or-business-associate-to-use-a-csp-that-stores-ephi-on-servers-outside-of-the-united-states/index.html), inspected 2026-10-14.
- **China HGR:** particular genetic resources/information and foreign access/international collaboration can invoke HGR permissions/filings and disclosure restrictions. Avoid treating every clinical/image/protein dataset as genetic-resource information; classify first. China's NHC published FAQs in **March 2025** (search-index evidence; direct fetch returned HTTP 412). [NHC HGR FAQs](https://www.nhc.gov.cn/qjjys/rlyczygl/202503/39a747744f2242068fdaf39b0452e11e.shtml); [NHC HGR regulations](https://www.nhc.gov.cn/bgt/gwywj2/201906/7f057bf005b44d87894e6764e73d557a.shtml). Other Chinese privacy/data transfer regimes must also be assessed separately; the cited HGR sources are not an exhaustive cross-border legal opinion.
- **Brazil:** ANPD Resolution 19 dated **2024-08-23** implements international-transfer mechanisms and standard contractual clauses under LGPD. [ANPD Resolution 19](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/institucional/atos-normativos/regulamentacoes_anpd/resolucao-cd-anpd-no-19-de-23-de-agosto-de-2024).

Recommended product controls: asset-level classification with steward review; allowed regions/processors/model providers enforced in jobs; tenant encryption/key controls; purpose-scoped access; approved redaction or de-identification transforms with measured residual risk; metadata-only/federated search options; no automatic cross-tenant learning; explicit rights before aggregated benchmarks or training; auditable transfer decisions and provenance. Metadata can itself be sensitive. Federated operation reduces movement but does not by itself satisfy transfer/privacy rules.

## Candidate differentiators grounded in these requirements

These are product hypotheses, not claims that competitors have never deployed them.

1. **Claim-to-evidence graph:** one intended-use or efficacy claim connects to evidence, exact result population, requirement and submitted versions. For IVDs, connect scientific validity, analytical and clinical performance separately. Surface claims with stale, contradictory or inapplicable evidence.
2. **Regulatory change impact:** a changed assay cutoff, software version, label claim, manufacturer/site or drug pack record identifies affected studies, risk controls, documents, IDMP/UDI records, filings and commitments across jurisdictions; experts approve consequences.
3. **Versioned jurisdiction rule registry:** immutable regulator source snapshot, requirement status (binding/guidance/draft/pilot), scope predicates, effective dates, validation package and local expert owner. Explain why a rule applied and retain the rules that governed an old filing.
4. **Evidence reuse with regional differences:** reuse approved evidence while preserving local language, product/indication, population and reference-regulator differences. Never equate reused content with automatic local acceptance.
5. **Reviewer rehearsal:** simulated deficiency questions tied to requirements and source evidence, with answerability scoring based on coverage and provenance. Present prioritised preparation findings; do not promise approval prediction.
6. **Reproducible submission builds:** generate from a frozen evidence baseline into eCTD, eSTAR-ready answer/attachment packages, IMDRF ToC, technical documentation and structured master-data proposals, then run deterministic validation and signed release.
7. **Consent-governed learning:** customer-controlled taxonomy/mapping improvements and aggregate process metrics when rights permit. A strong moat can come from reviewed mappings, valid adapters, accepted corrections and workflows; proprietary client evidence must not become a shared training corpus by default.

## Limits to preserve in the final strategic assessment

- Worldwide capability is an adapter roadmap, not a verified universal publishing engine. Representative markets do not cover every country, product type, expedited route, language or local rule.
- No source proves AI-generated dossiers will be accepted, replaces needed studies or permits missing evidence.
- Public regulator sources substantiate regulatory needs; novelty versus competitors requires separate competitor evidence, product access and patent/freedom-to-operate work where relevant.
- Optional/pilot/draft and future planned dates must remain visible in UI and marketing. Canada eSTAR page has a 2026 update but end-2025 eligibility; TGA v4 page still has 2025 pilot language. These stale-looking pages justify verification at implementation time, not unsupported assumptions of completed rollout.
