# Defensibility specification and customer comparison plan

Research and proposed pilot design, 14 October 2026. Read-only strategy work; no customer benchmark has been run. Platform evidence is pinned to commit `87b27c897c5d1be20b0527009bcaf425ebbf3af6` as recorded in the current audits. This plan does not enable deferred product scopes, certify regional filing support, predict approval or claim competitive exclusivity.

The most promising position is **a governed scientific-evidence workspace that makes evidence reuse, changes and filing claims inspectable inside the customer's existing systems**. Buyers already have document systems, RIM, QMS, laboratory records and publishing vendors. A durable advantage would come from expert-approved scientific relationships and reproducible decisions accumulated in their daily work, with measured improvements over the systems they already use.

## Existing foundation and prerequisites

Current source evidence supports immutable/hash-bound capture; attributed catalog suggestions; deterministic metadata and lexical/hybrid retrieval; a typed clinical-claim comparator; transaction-bound source checks and quote attribution; sample-size calculation persistence/replay; source usage and disposition impact; and accountable human action gates. These should be extended rather than replaced by a new AI subsystem. See [data-management assessment](current-data-management-assessment.md), [AnA audit](ana-code-audit.md) and [filing audit](filing-code-audit.md).

The same evidence identifies material limits: selected drafting reads document prefixes, catalog key labels do not establish scientific context, computation replay covers a narrow method, inserted calculated spans lack a first-class run/output reference, IVD/device fact frames need qualification, and many region/channel adapters are unqualified. Native XPT/SAS/Dataset-JSON and study-data bundle intake are founder-deferred scope. CSV/XLSX profiling is shape awareness, not standards conformance or an approved statistical pipeline.

Before client trials that depend on these hypotheses, qualify processing recovery, source-family identity, current source access, exact locator verification and stale-save refusal. A benchmark may deliberately expose a gap, but must record that outcome rather than route around it and call the feature ready. Use synthetic/client-authorized CSV/XLSX and retained documents first; separate future native study-data scope.

## Five differentiation hypotheses

### 1. Reuse evidence only when its scientific scope survives the new use

**Workflow.** A sponsor wants to reuse a study from another indication/population; a device manufacturer wants one verification report to cover another configuration; an IVD team wants serum performance evidence to support a plasma claim. The system proposes reuse with an explicit applicability rationale, exclusions and expert-approved bridging work. It distinguishes evidence that is reusable in an authoring workflow from evidence sufficient for a particular obligation. It never predicts that an agency will accept reuse.

**Durable asset and value.** Accepted applicability relationships between study/design/configuration, evidence, claim and purpose. A new program or market starts with inspectable prior decisions and the required bridging evidence, rather than repeatedly assembling the same review from documents. Public domain definitions can improve the product; private applicability decisions belong to the customer.

**Platform seam.** Extend current catalog facts and clinical frames with product/assay configuration, intended purpose, specimen/matrix, population and evidence purpose; use source locators and governed reviewer decisions. A general IVD/device applicability engine is proposed work, not current launch capability.

**Known competition and unknown depth.** Rimsys already connects product/market data and reuses submission content; Greenlight Guru links device design and risk records; Weave has source-connected global dossier reuse. The reviewed sources do not resolve how each system distinguishes scientifically valid reuse from superficially similar content in this exact scenario. Obtain a demonstrated answer. [Sourced comparison](competitors.md), [Rimsys platform](https://www.rimsys.io/products/rimsys-platform), [Weave global support](https://www.weave.bio/weave-bio-enables-global-submissions/).

**Head-to-head test.** Supply two similar study reports and six target uses. Some differ only in specimen, cutoff, population or device firmware. Ask each system which results can support each proposed claim and what smallest *expert-approved* set of bridging tasks resolves the uncovered obligations. Do not let an optimizer invent a study design or remove required evidence to reduce effort.

**Expert acceptance.** Every accepted reuse has the correct source version, frame and purpose; all seeded materially invalid reuses are refused or marked unresolved with the missing assumption named; safe reuse is identified rather than every task being refused. Bridging tasks match the adjudicated expert set or a documented acceptable alternative. Score reuse precision/recall, unsupported reuse, unnecessary new-evidence tasks and active review time separately.

**Rights and privacy.** A source may be usable by a person but unavailable for a new purpose/program. Enforce sponsor, program, purpose and licensed-use restrictions before retrieval and export. Do not share cross-sponsor applicability decisions or study content. Cross-program reuse within one customer also needs the customer's policy and accountable reviewer.

**Dependency, cost and adoption barrier.** Requires typed scientific identity, qualified mappings and domain experts; ambiguity is a normal result. Main costs are SME curation and long-tail assay/configuration vocabulary, not vector storage. Begin with one indication/configuration family; allow review of proposals where current metadata is incomplete. Integrating accepted reuse decisions into current review habits is more valuable than requiring customers to remodel every product first.

### 2. Make exclusions, denominators and unit choices reproducible evidence

**Workflow.** A CSR result changes after adjudication, a bench test excludes failed runs, or an IVD accuracy table omits indeterminate results. The published percentage may look correct while representing a different population, reference method, cutoff or exclusion policy. The platform carries the inclusion/exclusion policy and approved analysis frame into the calculation and the exact sentence/table cell that uses it.

**Durable asset and value.** Qualified analysis contracts, independently verified calculations, permitted transformations and reviewer resolutions. The asset is not a particular model's prose; it is the ability to reconstruct why a released result has that denominator and scope after upstream files change.

**Platform seam.** Extend existing canonical computation inputs/outputs, version hashes and narrow replay registry; add run/output references to current span lineage; qualify one existing IVD method and one pharma table first. Preserve raw data and approved SAP/method versions. Current dataset profiling alone does not supply this proof.

**Known competition and unknown depth.** Weave already has sentence/table source traces and automated verification; Benchling has cited scientific report preparation and scientific models; Certara/Artos have source-based authoring. Whether those exact workflows reconstruct exclusions and a calculation inserted into a released claim is not answered by a citation demo. Test this directly. [Sourced comparison](competitors.md), [Benchling report preparation](https://www.benchling.com/blog/benchling-ai-now-generally-available), [Weave verification](https://www.weave.bio/platform/submission-builder/).

**Head-to-head test.** Give a synthetic IVD cohort with positive/negative reference labels, indeterminate tests, duplicate specimens and a development/validation split. Supply an approved policy and an alternative unapproved policy yielding a plausible different percentage. Require a 2×2 result with its interval, denominator accounting, exact excluded-record references and claim provenance. In the pharma companion task, distinguish safety and efficacy populations with the same numeric rate. In the device task, distinguish a test invalidated by equipment failure from a product failure.

**Expert acceptance.** Independent reference calculations match within predeclared method/rounding tolerances; selected records and exclusions match the approved policy; no development cohort is presented as independent validation; units and method are retained. Every material released number links to run/output, source identities, policy and accepted decision. Unsupported method/design or missing policy must be explicit. Score calculation disagreement, context swaps, exclusion completeness and reproduction time; do not score a mere number's occurrence in a PDF as verification.

**Rights and privacy.** Raw subject/specimen data stays inside the approved customer/program environment; structural metadata and derived outputs are not automatically safe to share. Retain the minimal record references needed for audit under customer retention/legal-hold policy. Use synthetic data for vendor demonstrations and client data only under a written, purpose-bound pilot agreement.

**Dependency, cost and adoption barrier.** Statistical method qualification, governed input selection, units, exact locators and scientific reviewer availability are the gating costs. Native format readers remain a separately approved increment; integrate qualified existing computation/validation tools rather than write all methods. Adoption depends on respecting existing SAPs and validated statistical pipelines, not persuading teams to accept a new default calculation.

### 3. Preview the exact scientific consequences of a proposed change

**Workflow.** Before a sponsor changes an endpoint definition/data cut, a device team changes firmware or supplier, or an IVD team changes cutoff/reagent lot, ask what evidence, risks, calculations, claims, labels and pending responses require review. The preview is read-only. It explains dependency paths and separates actually affected content from similar but independent studies/configurations.

**Durable asset and value.** Expert-confirmed dependency paths and change/resolution histories. Repeated changes improve the coverage of known relationships and make future assessments more precise; the customer's scientific history becomes usable without silently changing approved records.

**Platform seam.** Reuse source-usage, checksum staleness, derived-impact and clinical comparison services. Add dependency-triggered rechecks and review tasks across scientific frames, computations and filing snapshots. Existing staleness/disposition machinery is a foundation, not evidence that this complete scenario engine is live.

**Known competition and unknown depth.** Rimsys and ArisGlobal already offer regulatory/product impact assessment; Greenlight Guru has design/risk/test links; Artos markets downstream document change relationships; Veeva connects quality and regulatory workflows. The test is fine-grained scientific consequences, including correct *unaffected* decisions, rather than whether a vendor has “impact analysis.” [Sourced comparison](competitors.md), [ArisGlobal agents](https://www.arisglobal.com/intelligence-agents/), [Artos downstream changes](https://www.artosai.com/feature/generative-ai-medical-writing-inconsistency-intelligence).

**Head-to-head test.** Introduce one cutoff change affecting only one assay/version; keep a different assay's identical values and filenames. Independently inject one source correction and one rule change. Ask for three separate impact previews, dependency explanations, proposed review tasks and exact proposed document differences. Then revoke access or withdraw the source before acceptance and verify that the proposal cannot apply from its stale preview.

**Expert acceptance.** Every seeded critical affected claim is found; named unaffected controls stay unaffected; unresolved edges are exposed instead of assigned invented certainty. Proposed edits preserve unrelated content, approvals and signatures. Old-preview acceptance, autonomous signing and cross-tenant impact exposure have zero tolerance in the finite challenge set. Score impact precision/recall, missed dependencies, unnecessary review tasks and review effort; a system refusing all scenarios does not demonstrate useful coverage.

**Rights and privacy.** A dependency graph must not leak another sponsor's filenames, project names or existence. Maintain authorization at preview, evidence retrieval and apply, including derived data in another permitted project. Preserve customer-controlled withdrawal/retention consequences; a retained derived result and deleted original are different states.

**Dependency, cost and adoption barrier.** Reliable source-family identity and accepted semantic edges are prerequisites. Costs rise with incremental recheck breadth and corpus size; benchmark a sparse affected set rather than rescan every document on every event. Review fatigue is the main adoption risk: optimize the precision of the work queue and show why each task exists.

### 4. Reconstruct what was filed and explain what became different afterward

**Workflow.** A reviewer asks why the Japan dossier used a different version from Europe, or why a current label no longer matches an earlier submitted table. The platform reconstructs the exact approved content, evidence, computations, rule versions and review receipts of a past filing, then compares the present state. It separates a requirement's effective date from when the organization learned or adopted it.

**Durable asset and value.** Immutable filing manifests and expert-reviewed interpretations tied to dates, applicability and outcomes. This history supports amendments, variations, responses, audit reconstruction and acquisition handoffs without rewriting past evidence to match current records.

**Platform seam.** Extend existing source versions, retained artifacts, audit lineage, persistent submission leaves and region readiness with pinned manifests and dated rule applicability. Approved prose is retained; hosted-model reruns are not promised to generate identical text. Non-US regional packaging/transmission is a qualification dependency, not a capability supplied by static country profiles.

**Known competition and unknown depth.** Veeva has submission archives/lifecycle management; LORENZ has sequence history; Rimsys has registrations, approvals and changes; Weave has version restore and global dossier continuity. Those are substantive overlaps. Public material does not establish or disprove exact replay of scientific calculation/effective-rule/known-at-date combinations. [Sourced comparison](competitors.md), [Veeva RIM](https://www.veeva.com/products/veeva-rim/), [LORENZ lifecycle](https://www.lorenz.cc/Solutions/docuBridge/).

**Head-to-head test.** Freeze a fictional filing, then change the raw dataset, reviewer decision and local rule pack on different dates. Introduce a transition window and a late-learned requirement. Ask for the filed artifact, contemporaneous evidence and rationale, current differences and proposed review work. Include a jurisdiction whose executable pack is unsupported: the system must state that boundary and offer an accurately scoped preparation handoff.

**Expert acceptance.** Filed bytes/hashes and retained approvals reconstruct exactly; calculation replay uses the pinned method; temporal applicability matches an expert-approved fixture. No retroactive rewrite of prior evidence or unsupported present-tense “compliant/fileable” verdict. Correct preparation, technical packaging, validator qualification and agency acknowledgement are separately reported. Score reconstruction success, temporal mistakes, unsupported coverage claims and audit-answer effort.

**Rights and privacy.** Manifest access follows source/artifact rights and legal holds. Retained client filings and agency correspondence cannot become another client's examples. License regulatory standards and translations; public laws/guidance still require faithful attribution and versioning. Historical visibility does not override a person's revoked access.

**Dependency, cost and adoption barrier.** Requires trustworthy artifact storage, rule ownership, source locators, reviewer records and qualified adapters. Most cost is regional expert maintenance and validation/transport licensing; duplicate storage is secondary. Start with historical reconstruction of an existing US workflow and document-based regional handoffs. Do not require clients to replace the established publisher before demonstrating value.

### 5. Turn accepted review corrections into governed regression cases

**Workflow.** An SME corrects an IVD context swap, a regulatory writer rejects a misleading emphasis, or an agency question reveals a recurring missing justification. The system records the error type, original source/context, accepted resolution and applicability. A future proposal or model/retrieval/rule upgrade is tested against the case before entering that customer's workflow; the correction repairs shared context where appropriate, not just one answer.

**Durable asset and value.** Customer-specific, adjudicated scientific/regulatory evaluation cases and reusable accepted resolutions. The asset compounds through ordinary review, but remains bounded by program/purpose and expires when its factual basis changes. It improves prevention and review efficiency without requiring customer model training.

**Platform seam.** Reuse attributed suggestions, human confirmation, quote/claim comparison, governed actions and versioned audit provenance. Add structured correction categories and held-out evaluation receipts. A proposed “learning loop” is not authorization to fine-tune, pool tenants or expand deferred self-evolving engines.

**Known competition and unknown depth.** Alation publicly describes evaluation/context-fix loops; Collibra has model/agent lifecycle governance; Weave and ArisGlobal reuse agency responses and preserve expert review. The differentiation hypothesis is domain-specific prevention with bounded applicability and evidence of non-regression, not the existence of feedback. [Sourced comparison](competitors.md), [Alation agents](https://www.alation.com/aios/agents/), [Collibra governance documentation](https://productresources.collibra.com/docs/collibra/2026.08/Content/AIGovernance/co_about-ai-governance.htm).

**Head-to-head test.** Teach one accepted correction on a training example. Test held-out same-context variants, different-context examples where reuse would be wrong, a withdrawn source and a later method/rule change. Repeat after an approved model/retrieval version update. Ask whether the correction propagates to appropriate future proposals and whether another customer's project remains unaffected.

**Expert acceptance.** The target error is prevented on the held-out applicable cohort; different context is not overwritten; outdated corrections are reevaluated or refused; no protected content crosses customer boundaries. Compare recurrence counts, new-error counts, reviewer acceptance and active review time. Retest against a frozen control cohort; a single corrected demo is not evidence of learning or release quality.

**Rights and privacy.** Private corrections, responses and factual examples remain customer-owned. Cross-client improvement uses public/licensed/synthetic material or separately permissioned, reviewed contributions with tested disclosure controls. Agreement to use the product is not agreement to pool correspondence or train models. Customers need export/deletion/retention controls for their evaluation cases.

**Dependency, cost and adoption barrier.** Requires a lightweight capture workflow, expert adjudication and stable evaluation manifests. Labeling and running affected regression cohorts are the costs; excessive correction forms discourage adoption. Record essential facts during existing review, ask the expert to approve applicability, and only expand the corpus where measured recurrence justifies it.

National/device-class differences and Notified Body processes are selected by the customer's local experts. ### Fixture design and reference answers

Proposed complete matrix: 15 product/market cells, each with six task variants, for **90 planned task cases**. This is an evaluation design count, not a claim of completed tests or statistically proven real-world reliability. Begin with three US/EU science fixtures to qualify shared semantics, then extend to the full matrix.

Each cell contains:

1. Clean evidence with an answerable, exactly scoped claim and accepted reuse.
2. A lookalike study/configuration/specimen carrying the same number, filename or phrase but a different scientific frame.
3. Decisive evidence late in a long report, with a table footnote and a scan/translation uncertainty requiring an explicit coverage decision.
4. A changed data cut, cutoff, source or configuration affecting a sparse subset; include unaffected controls.
5. A historical filing with rule effective/learned dates and a transition-window decision.
6. Permission revocation, withdrawal or stale proposal between review and save/export, including another tenant/program with a tempting matching source.

Generate values and metadata independently from the product implementation. Reference calculations come from a separately qualified method/tool with predeclared conventions. One scientific SME and one local regulatory/quality SME independently label material claims, valid reuse, expected affected nodes, necessary evidence and support boundaries; a third expert resolves disagreement. Keep uncertainty and disagreement visible. Freeze source bytes, expected source spans/cells, methods, rules, expected/forbidden outcomes and difficulty labels before product runs.

Include bounded dossier sizes and larger stress cases; record bytes, pages, tables and processing/OCR coverage. Retrieval acceptance pins nonzero exact UTF-16 source spans and the full extracted-text SHA-256, with table title, multilevel headers and footnote context. Measure indexed-source coverage, retrieval coverage and serialized-model-context coverage separately; source-selection budgets and model-message budgets are different constraints. Do not credit relevant-range grounding while claim offsets still assume prefixes. Reuse the current lexical/hybrid path rather than introduce a parallel RAG store. Japanese cases require a competent bilingual reviewer and preserve numerals, units and original-language context. Translation fluency alone is not a passing scientific result. Use appropriately licensed source/standard excerpts and equivalent evidence packages across products; do not upload client material into a vendor demo without permission and approved processing terms.

### Fair head-to-head comparison

Compare the current customer workflow first, then Concept2Cure and the relevant installed or licensed alternatives. Candidate pairings: pharma authoring against Weave plus the customer's Veeva/ArisGlobal/Certara workflow; device/IVD against Rimsys and Greenlight Guru/MasterControl; scientific preparation against Benchling where it already holds the source record. Choose a small finalist set per workflow; testing every vendor on every case wastes integration/SME effort and obscures differences.

Record the vendor/product/version, GA/beta/preview state, actual licensed modules, configuration, source ingestion success, operator training, integrations and allowed customization. Score the complete deployed workflow where it combines tools, including human/publishing effort. A preview workflow can be evaluated separately but must retain its status. A capability unavailable to the test team is **not assessed**, not “competitor lacks it.” Vendor participation and authorized account access are customer-managed future steps; none are requested or performed by this document.

Use the same evidence, target outputs and review rubric, with trained operators and equivalent preparation. Randomize task order and blind reviewers to the producing system where possible. Run AI cases repeatedly to reveal variability, preserving outputs and model/retrieval settings; run count is agreed in the protocol and includes cost. Keep a held-out challenge cohort inaccessible to prompt/template designers. A paired crossover helps distinguish tool effects from operator familiarity; report learning and configuration effort separately from recurring review effort.

### Acceptance and scorecard

These proposed criteria require written SME/client agreement before the pilot. They are finite release/test gates, not universal reliability claims.

| Dimension | Expert-accepted result | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Material factual support | Correct scientific frame and resolvable exact source/run references for every material accepted claim | Accepted-claim support rate; wrong-source/frame count; unresolved rate |
| Critical seeded hazards | Every predefined critical invalid reuse/context/temporal/permission condition is blocked or correctly marked unresolved; unsupported/unread cannot be called verified | Critical escapes by category; coverage and false refusal reported separately |
| Reproducible numbers | Independent result matches declared method/rounding tolerance; approved inclusion/exclusion and units are preserved | Calculation error; exclusion completeness; replay success/time |
| Reuse and change usefulness | Adjudicated safe reuse and affected nodes found; unrelated controls retained | Precision/recall and unnecessary tasks; retain per-case counts, not just aggregate accuracy |
| Historical truth | Retained artifact hashes/decisions reconstruct; present changes do not alter past record | Exact reconstruction; temporal/applicability mistakes |
| Authorization | No unauthorized source content, identifiers or graph existence is exposed; stale access cannot save/export | Cross-tenant/program/purpose leak count and stale-acceptance count: zero in challenge set |
| Workflow quality | Local SME accepts scoped outputs after accounted review; rendering/packaging/channel status is honest | Review revisions, active SME minutes, refusal/unresolved distribution |
| Learning | Applicable held-out recurrence falls without new failures in protected/different-context controls | Before/after error counts and paired review effort |
| Operational cost | Record ingestion, OCR, compute/model cost and human/integration effort for the entire workflow | Cost per expert-accepted task; p50/p95 elapsed/active time; retries and queue load |

Set noncritical quantitative improvement targets *after* measuring the current workflow; do not invent ROI. Report paired task results and uncertainty, stratified by product, jurisdiction, language and hazard; do not pool away a weak market or critical miss. If the small cohort is inconclusive, collect more examples before claiming superiority. A technically valid artifact is not scientific sufficiency or agency approval. No approval probability metric is proposed.

## Build, buy and reuse decisions

| Capability | Recommendation | Reason / commercial constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Typed scientific facts, applicability, accepted resolution history | **Build on current contracts** with domain SME corpora | This is product-specific defensibility; avoid a second catalog or silent reinterpretation of approved legacy facts |
| Source identity, authorization, hashing, lineage, retrieval, audit and governed actions | **Reuse and harden existing platform** | Current spine already supplies much of the control; a new graph/vector platform is justified only by measured failure of the current store/query path |
| Calculations and native scientific-format readers | **Integrate qualified tools/libraries; build contracts and validation evidence** | Standard statistics/parsers are seldom the moat. Check method coverage, licenses, reproducibility, maintenance and privacy; native format expansion remains separately authorized |
| OCR, table geometry and translation | **Buy/integrate selectively**, preserving original locators/confidence | Language/scan quality is costly. Provider processing region/retention and recoverable evidence are mandatory; no model-generated cell can become verified by default |
| eCTD/eSTAR regional publishing, specialist validation and gateways | **Integrate qualified publishers/validators and official templates**; build bounded adapter truth | Agency/vendor protocols and licensing are significant maintenance obligations. Client portal handoff is valid where qualified direct transport is absent |
| Domain rule packs and standards | **Curate internally with licensed/local partners** | Expert interpretation, effective dates, jurisdiction distinctions and conformance fixtures need ownership; scraped text is not a validated rule pack |
| Model inference | **Use approved provider gateway and current control contracts** | Proprietary model training is unnecessary for first value; context/rights/test evidence are more durable. Any new dependency follows repository justification and qualification rules |

## Buyer and enterprise integration position

**Primary buyer for the first sale:** the accountable Regulatory Operations leader for a defined pharma, device or IVD program/portfolio, with the scientific/quality owner as joint acceptance authority. Economic sponsor is typically the corresponding regulatory/R&D/quality executive. Sell one costly evidence-change/reuse workflow that the buyer owns; do not lead with replacing all enterprise data management or building a universal global filing AI.

**Initial enterprise footprint:** one source system connector, one program/tenant, one accepted scientific frame and one existing authoring/publishing handoff. Favor read-only import with source-system identifiers/version/hash, incremental sync and existing SSO/role mapping. Keep Vault/RIM/QMS/PLM/EDC/LIMS/statistical systems as the customer's established records where appropriate. Write back a reviewed result, review task or approved document only through a bounded, auditable connector; connector recommendations here are not claims of present integration.

An acceptable early customer experience is: identify a proposed change → show the precisely affected evidence and content → reviewer verifies sources/computation/context → reviewer accepts a patch/task → export into the established workflow with its evidence receipt. Show task status, uncertainty and support boundary at the decision point. Hide coding/model/graph internals unless needed for a meaningful scientific or operational decision.

**Integration acceptance:** stable source identity; no silent same-title supersession; tenant/program/purpose rights; resolved conflict ownership; stale save/export refusal; audit receipt; retry/idempotency; recoverable storage; agreed retention/legal holds; and a reproducible accepted artifact. Review enterprise data placement and subprocessors before real-data ingestion. No external training, federated pooling or autonomous signatures are implied.

**Commercial proof package:** a protocol agreed with the customer, exact scoped capability/status register, adjudicated fixture manifest, paired benchmark results, observed workflow costs, material failure/unknowns and a bounded rollout decision. Customer-owned evidence relationships and exports should remain portable. Retention earned through scientific usefulness, verified reliability and integration is stronger than artificial switching barriers.

## Proposed decision order

1. Qualify capture/retrieval/save controls and measure the existing customer workflow.
2. Implement/test one context-qualified IVD or clinical fact, one reproducible calculation reference and one accepted reuse decision using authorized formats.
3. Demonstrate precise change preview and historical reconstruction on that program with existing publishing handoff.
4. Add a customer-owned correction/evaluation loop and rerun held-out cases after upgrades.
5. Expand to the 15-cell market matrix only when local expert/adapter coverage is explicit; deferred dataset formats and new filing scopes remain separate increments.

The criterion for choosing the next increment is demonstrated recurring client burden plus achievable scientific qualification. Neither competitor feature lists nor internal module names establish a moat; the approved relationships, difficult regression evidence, dependable integrations and measured customer outcomes may do so.
