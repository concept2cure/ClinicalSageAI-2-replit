migrations/20260507_mdx_beta_surfaces.sql and migrations/20260511_qms_and_labeling.sql, and adds the same-organization key
that migrations/20261013b_qms_inspection_same_org_keys.sql adds to labeling_documents.program_id. All three are on C2C_MIGRATION_FILES.
regulatory_programs is reduced to the columns the keys read (id, organization_id, deleted_at).
