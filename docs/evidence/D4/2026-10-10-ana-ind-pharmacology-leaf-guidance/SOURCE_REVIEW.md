# IND pharmacology leaf source review

Reviewed 2026-10-10 UTC (2026-10-09 Pacific). This D4 authoring enhancement
promotes seven existing structural headings into exact, evidence-limited
authoring records in the existing AnA drafting path. The review establishes
the scope of those records; it does not establish that a sponsor's IND,
scientific evidence, model, source-retrieval path or filing is qualified.

## Exact numbering and bounded content

The following mapping is confirmed in final FDA M4S, printed pages 14–15
(PDF pages 18–19), and ICH M4S(R2), printed pages 5–6 (PDF pages 9–10).

| Code | Exact heading | Bounded content scope |
|---|---|---|
| 2.6.2.1 | Brief Summary | Describe the available pharmacology package, principal findings and notable exclusions or missing models. The M4S suggestion of approximately 2–3 pages is not a universal initial-IND completeness rule. |
| 2.6.2.2 | Primary Pharmacodynamics | Summarize and evaluate studies concerning the intended therapeutic target. Use class comparisons only when supported by available evidence. |
| 2.6.2.3 | Secondary Pharmacodynamics | Evaluate effects beyond the intended target, organized by organ system when useful. |
| 2.6.2.4 | Safety Pharmacology | Evaluate available functional safety findings, including relevant secondary studies, and their significance for potential human adverse effects. |
| 2.6.2.5 | Pharmacodynamic Drug Interactions | Summarize interaction studies when performed; missing evidence is not a negative finding. |
| 2.6.2.6 | Discussion and Conclusions | Discuss the pharmacologic evaluation, limitations and significant unresolved issues without unsupported conclusions. |
| 2.6.2.7 | Tables and Figures | Use supplied, traceable displays within the narrative or at its end. A separate display is not invariably necessary. |

These are leaves of the 2.6.2 written summary. Its displays do not replace the
separate 2.6.3 pharmacology tabulated summary. Pharmacodynamic interactions
belong here; pharmacokinetic interaction summaries belong under 2.6.4.7.
The corresponding study-report locations are 4.2.1.1–4.2.1.4 for primary PD,
secondary PD, safety pharmacology and PD interactions respectively. The
broader integrated nonclinical overview remains 2.4.

## IND and modality scope

[21 CFR 312.23(a)(8)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-312/subpart-B/section-312.23)
requires adequate pharmacology/toxicology support for the proposed clinical
investigation. Testing scope and duration depend on that investigation. It
also addresses qualified evaluators, study locations, inspectable records
and safety-related informational amendments during development. The eCFR
page displayed currency through 2026-10-07 when reviewed. Its then-current
animal/in-vitro terminology must not be silently replaced with a future
rule's wording.

M3(R2), section II, generally places cardiovascular, CNS and respiratory
core safety evaluations before human exposure; warranted follow-up work
can occur later. Primary PD studies commonly arise during discovery and
are generally non-GLP. For biotechnology-derived products, M3(R2) points
to S6 for study selection and principally addresses timing itself.

S7A, sections 2.9–2.11, permits justified product-specific approaches and
appropriately designed endpoints within other studies. Core safety studies
ordinarily follow GLP; justified exceptions still need reliable data and
adequate documentation. An absent uploaded report does not establish that
a study was unnecessary, unperformed or free of findings.

The [FDA Phase 1 FAQ](https://www.fda.gov/industry/phase-1-investigational-new-drug-ind-navigator/phase-1-ind-frequently-asked-questions)
ties nonclinical information to population, modality and mechanism. No
therapy-area label alone establishes the applicable program. For example,
[final S9](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/s9-nonclinical-evaluation-anticancer-pharmaceuticals)
has an advanced-cancer/limited-treatment-options scope, and
[final FDA cellular/gene-therapy preclinical guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/preclinical-assessment-investigational-cellular-and-gene-therapy-products)
provides distinct product-specific recommendations. This batch does not
implement or qualify those complete modality-specific study programs.

scientifically justified alternative methods must still be assessed for the
particular context; this receipt neither bans all alternatives nor approves
every proposed NAM.

## Authoring safeguards inferred from this review

These are implementation safeguards, not additional study mandates:

- Preserve supplied development phase, modality, route, population, duration
  and evidence cutoff. Missing context remains unresolved.
- Distinguish not supplied, not performed, planned, deferred and not
  applicable. Do not infer the latter states without explicit support.
- Trace factual results to study/report identifiers and supplied locations.
  Preserve units, uncertainty and any species/model limitations.
- Do not invent results, sample sizes, exposure margins, GLP status,
  interaction absence, starting doses or favorable safety conclusions.
- Treat modality and therapeutic-area declarations as advisory context;
  neither verifies evidence nor substitutes for scientific review.
- Do not mark all seven sections or all study types universally mandatory
  for every initial IND. Unknown applicability remains an open question.

Static source references help authoring and do not become qualified
scientific basis merely by being listed. Reviewing these links does not
automatically update embedded platform references, activate continuous
source monitoring, qualify a model, establish every therapy/modality
combination, or clear an IND for filing. The implemented catalogue and
gateway tests must be reported separately from scientific qualification.

No blocking source-scope discrepancy was found. In particular, the records
retain conditional study status, appropriate S7A/S6 boundaries, PD/PK

into currently effective requirements. This is a bounded semantic review
of static instructions, not an evaluation of generated scientific prose or
a sponsor's submission.
