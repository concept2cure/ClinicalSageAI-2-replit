# IND source review

Reviewed 2026-10-09 UTC (2026-10-08 Pacific). These source observations guide the
coverage audit; they do not upgrade every embedded platform statement to verified.
No regulator text is copied into model prompts by this delivery.

| Primary source | Observation relevant to the implementation plan |
|---|---|
| [21 CFR 312.23](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-312/subpart-B/section-312.23) | IND content includes administrative information, the investigational plan, applicable investigator brochure, protocols, CMC, nonclinical information and prior human experience. Detail depends on the investigation and phase; an unknown applicability condition cannot be treated as an exclusion. |
| [FDA Phase 1 IND Navigator](https://www.fda.gov/industry/phase-1-investigational-new-drug-ind-navigator) | FDA provides distinct guidance, CMC flexibility, procedure and regulatory resources for early development. The plan must support phase-specific content instead of prescribing a marketing dossier for every initial IND. |
| [FDA Phase 1 guidance index](https://www.fda.gov/industry/phase-1-investigational-new-drug-ind-navigator/phase-1-ind-guidance-documents) | The index identifies dates and draft/final status. For example, it lists M11 as final in May 2026 and genome-editing prior-knowledge guidance as draft in June 2026. Product-specific guidance cannot be presumed final from its title. |
| [FDA eCTD standards](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/electronic-regulatory-submission-and-review/ectd-submission-standards-ectd-v322-and-regional-m1) | Versions and support dates are separately listed. The page records an August 28, 2026 validation-criteria update. Source publication date, support date and requirement date are distinct facts. |
| [FDA eCTD overview](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/electronic-regulatory-submission-and-review/electronic-common-technical-document-ectd) | Both supported versions must be checked against the current catalog. Noncommercial INDs differ from commercial INDs in electronic-submission requirements. A new v4.0 application is not equivalent to converting an existing sequence. |
| [FDA human gene therapy IND CMC guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/chemistry-manufacturing-and-control-cmc-information-human-gene-therapy-investigational-new-drug) | The final January 2020 guidance addresses gene-therapy CMC, including relevant combination products. Modality-specific content is a separate dimension from disease area. |
| [FDA ICH S9](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/s9-nonclinical-evaluation-anticancer-pharmaceuticals) | The final guidance's stated scope is anticancer drugs for advanced disease with limited therapeutic options. An oncology label alone does not establish that scope. |

The coverage roadmap is an engineering inference from these sources and the
repository audit. This session has not reviewed every therapeutic-area guidance,
every CTD content requirement or any sponsor's deployed submission. It does not
establish universal IND subject-matter expertise or regulatory acceptance.
