## Behaviour pinned (not regulatory facts)

- IND, NDA and BLA required sets are identical before and after the move. HEAD and the working copy were compared with `quickValidate([], t)` for IND, NDA, BLA, ANDA, ind and nda: all the same. MAA went from empty (not assessed) to the EU profile. See `g-required-sections-home-green.txt`.
- A region that is given and is not the type's home region now returns null instead of the FDA set. Examples are an NDA validated with region EU or CA, and an IND validated for JP.
- Fix round 1: IND (the default submission type of the export validate/preflight routes and of the MCP tool) with a region of EMA, PMDA, CA or any other non-FDA region is now **not assessed** (a `REQUIRED_SECTIONS_NOT_ASSESSED` warning). Before, it was judged against the FDA IND set. So `valid` can now be true on an empty package with no type given and a non-FDA region. The legacy `MISSING_REGIONAL_*` check still runs.
