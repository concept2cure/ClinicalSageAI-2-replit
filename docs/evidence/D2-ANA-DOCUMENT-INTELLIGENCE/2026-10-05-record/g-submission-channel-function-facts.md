## Scope of the eSubmission Gateway rule (fix round 2)

Row 1 names centralised-procedure eCTD submissions: new MAAs, variations, renewals, PSURs and ASMFs/PMFs. `submissionChannelFor` therefore applies the eSubmission Gateway channel and the "Centralised procedure" reason only to EMA-agency entries with `submissionFormat: 'eCTD'` in one of these families: `marketing_authorization`, `variation`, `renewal`, `safety_report` (EU_RMP, EU_PSUR) and `master_file` (EU_ASMF). Round 1 used "EU + EMA + eCTD" and also caught orphan, PIP, PRIME and scientific advice. That claimed the centralised procedure for filings that are not centralised-procedure eCTD submissions.

## Product decision this step implements

DECISIONS.md row 10 and ANA_REGULATORY_RECORD.md §12 item 10 / §13 apply here. No EMA eSubmission Gateway (AS2) connector is built (Rule 2). A centralised MAA is **build-only**: the platform builds and validates the eCTD sequence and names the channel, and the applicant transmits it there.
