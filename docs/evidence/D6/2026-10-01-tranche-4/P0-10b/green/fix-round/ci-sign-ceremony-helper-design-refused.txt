Captured 2026-10-01 during this fix round (first design, not shipped).

The first version of server/api/cmc/cmc-signer.ts moved verifyReauth out of the
three CMC handlers into a shared verifyCmcSigner(). ci:sign-ceremony proves a
`sign` write is ceremonied by finding verifyReauth/reverifySigner in the same
handler, so it refused all three sites:

===== npm run -s ci:sign-ceremony
[ci:sign-ceremony] FAIL — a `sign` ledger write without the signature ceremony:
  ✗ server/api/cmc/batchRecordRoutes.ts:481  (no verifyReauth/reverifySigner)
  ✗ server/api/cmc/routes.ts:903  (no verifyReauth/reverifySigner)
  ✗ server/api/cmc/specificationRoutes.ts:379  (no verifyReauth/reverifySigner)
exit=1

The shipped design keeps verifyReauth in each handler and moves only the
authority check (refusedWithoutSigningAuthority) and the factor record
(verifiedReauthFactors) into cmc-signer.ts. ci:sign-ceremony then passes
(gates.txt).
