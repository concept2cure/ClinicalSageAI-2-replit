Why the phone run stopped at Send (2026-10-30). Measured in Chromium at 390x844,
isMobile + hasTouch, on the private instance, before any fix.

1. The page is wider than the screen on the Authoring surface only:
     login                      innerWidth 390  scrollWidth 390  clientWidth 390
     home                       innerWidth 390  scrollWidth 390  clientWidth 390
     document-authoring         innerWidth 485  scrollWidth 485  clientWidth 390
   A phone browser widens the layout viewport to the page: 485 x 1048.

2. Send for review, open on that page (position: fixed, so laid out on 485 x 1048):
     .de-bd   backdrop          x=0    y=0    w=485  h=1048
     .de      the sheet         x=118  y=0    w=367  h=1048
     .de-f    its footer        x=119  y=971  w=366  h=77
     sfr-submit (Send)          x=308  y=988  w=157  h=44
   The visible screen is 390 x 844, so the footer sits below it and the sheet runs
   95px past its right edge. Playwright's click scrolled and then hit the form's
   labels (fail-detail.txt in 1-script-as-filed/).

3. What makes the page 485px wide. Hiding body's descendants one at a time:
     div.c2c-v2.shell > main.main > div.page > div.ed > section.ed-doc
       > header.ed-doc-h > div.ed-doc-actions (overflow-x:auto, scrollWidth 2543)
   and inside the toolbar the one positioned box is
     span.sr-only (position:absolute, 1x1)  x=483  right=484  parent span.dlm
   the Download menu's live region. `.ed-doc-actions` is position:static, so it is
   not that box's containing block and does not clip it: the box stays at its
   place in the unscrolled row and extends the page to 485px.

4. Why the script said "horizontal overflow 0px": it measured
   scrollWidth - innerWidth = 485 - 485. On a mobile viewport innerWidth is the
   widened layout viewport. 2-overflow-measured/ is the same run with the overflow
   measured against the screen's 390px: "horizontal scroll of 95px".
