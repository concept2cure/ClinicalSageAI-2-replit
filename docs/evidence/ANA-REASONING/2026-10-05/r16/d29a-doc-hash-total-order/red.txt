D-29-A — RED BEFORE GREEN
=========================

The case: `computeDocHash` read `authoring_sections` with `ORDER BY order_index`
and no tiebreaker, while the digest it feeds (`sectionsDigest`) states in its own
docstring that the rows must already be in "the caller's recorded order", and
`fullVisibleAuthoringSnapshot` names that order: "Rows use signing's total
order" — `order_index, created_at, id`.

The assertion that fails is the invariant itself: the hash a signature stores
IS the digest of the text the signing route read.
