P-29.2 INCREMENT 1 — MUTANTS
============================

Each mutant is applied to the gate, the nine P-29 cases run, and reverted.
"killed" = the suite fails.

 G1  refuse on EVERY divergence, including `empty`      KILLED (1 case)
 G2  refuse only on `empty`, never on `different`        KILLED (4 cases)
 G3  compare trimmed text instead of exact bytes         KILLED (1 case)
 G4  never report a divergence at all                    KILLED (3 cases)
 G5  drop the "nothing authored yet" guard               KILLED (1 case)
 G6  widen the scope to every node of the filing         KILLED (8 of 9 cases)

 BASE                                                    9 passed

G3 and G5 are why the suite has nine cases rather than seven. A trimmed
comparison and a missing empty-guard both survived the first seven, and each is
a real failure: a §11.70 digest over "…</p> " does not match one over "…</p>",
so a whitespace-only difference is still text the signature does not cover; and
reporting every un-typed section as "the filing does not hold this" would make
the signal meaningless on every document under construction.

G6 is the one worth reading. Widening the comparison to every filing node
breaks EIGHT of the nine cases — which is the review's own warning
("it must be scoped to the nodes the document maps to or it refuses every
freeze") shown rather than asserted. A filing node no section of this document
reaches may belong to another document of the same filing, which is the
ordinary shape of a multi-document submission.

NOT KILLED BY A MUTANT, AND WHY NOT, STATED RATHER THAN GLOSSED
---------------------------------------------------------------

"Run the gate AFTER the seal." The "nothing was sealed" assertions exist for
this, and no single-point mutation can exercise them: the route's
`finally { if (!committed) ROLLBACK }` means a gate placed anywhere before the
COMMIT rolls its writes back regardless, so every such mutant is equivalent.
The assertions would catch a gate moved BELOW the COMMIT, which is a
restructuring rather than a mutation. Recorded as a pin whose mutant does not
exist, not as a mutant that was killed.

"Pass the pool instead of the caller's transaction client." Observing it needs
a concurrent writer between the check and the seal, which this harness has no
way to schedule. The reason it matters is stated at the function instead.
