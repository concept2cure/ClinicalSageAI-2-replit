The new refusal reason, measured rather than assumed.

Round 13 measured its two refusal reasons at 5.21:1 (light) and 6.82:1 (dark) in
real Chromium under the shipped stylesheets. Door 5's reason uses the same values
(12px, var(--text-300)) but sits in a DIFFERENT container — the artifact card,
`.ct-art`, which declares its own background — so round 13's measurement does not
carry over by assumption. A token that is AA on one surface can fail on another;
that is exactly what round 13's capture found in lineage/palette.ts, whose "AA"
claim had only ever been measured in light mode.

Surface, from app-v2.css:2921:
  .c2c-v2 .ct-art{ ... background:var(--bg-000); ... }

Tokens, from design-system/colors_and_type.css (:root and .dark/[data-theme=dark]):
  light  --text-300 #6b6963   --bg-000 #faf9f5
  dark   --text-300 #b0aea5   --bg-000 #262624

Computed with the repo's own single copy of the arithmetic, scripts/lib/wcag.mjs
(contrastRatio over sRGB triples):

  light  --text-300 on --bg-000    5.21:1   PASS (AA, normal text, needs 4.5:1)
  dark   --text-300 on --bg-000    6.82:1   PASS

Both figures match round 13's measured pair exactly, which also establishes what
was not previously written down: the conversation's refusal reason sits on the
same --bg-000 surface.
