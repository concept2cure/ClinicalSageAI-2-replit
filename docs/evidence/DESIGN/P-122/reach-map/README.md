# Reach map: the 59 screens locked outside the launch catalog

Read-only investigation of `/home/user/ClinicalSageAI-2-replit`, branch `concept2cure-v2`, read between
`acbcf378e` and `d41ef02b9` on 2026-11-11. Nothing in the repository was changed. No server, vitest or tsc was run.

## 1. The answer in short

- **The 59 locked screens are not 59 lost capabilities.** 8 are already delivered by a launch screen. 32 are
  duplicates, reference pages or shells whose own engine is missing, off, or has no way to enter data. 11 hold a real
  engine that a launch screen should carry. 8 are outside the catalog and need the founder.
- **The capability a paying team is missing today is concentrated in about ten places:** the nonclinical study
  register (so Module 2.4/2.6 and Module 4 have data), the trial master file, the Investigator's Brochure readiness
  check, the device change determination, deep research, precedent search, AnA's commitment tools, 16 Global RI
  engines, regional Module 1, and later registrations and NDA/BLA. IND safety reporting is the one founder decision
  with high value for the launch customer.
- **Unlocking a screen would not deliver most of these.** For 17 of the 59, a store the screen reads has no migration
  on the deploy path (section 2). 18 make at least one call that no surface claims, so production would refuse it
  even with the screen unlocked.
- **Most of what is unreached is not in these screens at all.** The R1 inventory
  (`docs/evidence/DESIGN/R1/2026-11-01-inventory/`) counted 3,923 API handlers: 341 served with no caller, 182 called
  but refused, and 171 route files with no caller. AnA's inventory withholds 117 tools and 20 commands filed under
  locked screens (`server/services/ana/ana-launch-scope.inventory.json`), 77 of them research administration.
