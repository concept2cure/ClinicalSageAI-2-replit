## Measured work that remains

The complete request automation needs verified capture/binding of repeated questions and other request-specific fields, authentic attachment behavior, applicant declarations and independently observed interactive completion. FDA's actual Questions and SIR attachment scripts append literal `|XXX` tokens rather than a resolved chapter; the current canonical chapter planner deliberately refuses/omits those unqualified routes. Their exact script excerpts are in the inspection record. A chapter must not be invented from a label.

The official template hides its administrative/question sections until applicant-driven XFA events reveal them. pdf.js does not execute these scripts, so the independent render check explicitly demonstrates that the hidden administrative sentinel is absent from its initial rendered tree. It proves independent parsing and attachment retrieval, not Adobe's interactive behavior or display of hidden fields. The FDA email exit scripts also use a legacy two-to-three-letter-TLD expression; binding a supplied email does not prove its acceptance under those interactive scripts. No secured FDA script was altered to bypass these constraints.

These limits are concrete remaining engineering/interactive qualification requirements. They are separate from sponsor credentials or agency UAT. Full PreSTAR automated filing remains blocked in filing readiness and the GA report, including when registration, substantive content, current template and shared administrative mappings are all present. No agency receipt or acceptance was created.
