# O01 continuation: administrative configuration and legal acceptance

The continuation preserves the existing finance, organization and legal authority boundaries. It adds no commercial capability, UI surface, store or dependency. Six previously discarded audit outcomes are addressed at their actual execution paths.

| Existing operation | Audit policy and observable result |
| --- | --- |
| Business Center cost-rate and tier-price edits | The database save already completed. `recordAuditRow` supplies `meta.auditTrail` alongside the existing saved row. Failed or unexpectedly throwing audit writers cannot turn a completed edit into a retryable HTTP 500. |
| Business Center CSV export | The bytes remain CSV; `setAuditRowHeaders` supplies the canonical `X-Audit-Row-Persisted` / failure-code headers. |
| Purchased-seat and weekly-limit configuration | The service returns its saved configuration plus `AuditRowOutcome`. The two actual handlers in `billing-dashboard.ts` forward it as `meta.auditTrail`; raw audit-store error text stays off the wire. The seat setter uses the actual allocation from `UPDATE ... RETURNING` and its validated count snapshot, avoiding a second metering read that could fail after the allocation saved. |
| Legal agreement acceptance | Acceptance insertion and canonical `writeChainedAuditRow` execute on the same transaction connection. Failure of either rolls back both. The legal act cannot complete with a detached best-effort audit mirror. A share lock retains the agreement version while that transaction records acceptance. |

An acceptance whose `COMMIT` acknowledgement is lost now returns the explicit `LICENSE_ACCEPTANCE_COMMIT_UNCONFIRMED` HTTP 503 with the existing `/api/licensing/acceptances` recovery URL and a direction to review history before trying again. It does not claim rollback, return `accepted:true`, or attempt an automatic retry. The uncertain connection is discarded. A regression executes the real commit and then loses its acknowledgement, proving the acceptance and sealed audit remain discoverable together.

The continuation also repairs a demonstrated violation of the service's existing scope contract: individual agreements are satisfied only by that user's acceptance, while organization agreements are satisfied only by acceptance for the selected organization. The former unfiltered union allowed a colleague's individual EULA to satisfy another user and allowed an actor's organization acceptance to follow them into another tenant. The reads now join the canonical agreement scope; tests prove both refusals and both legitimate positive paths. This does not decide which role may bind an organization or mount the inactive enforcement gate.

The existing `logAuditEvent` wrappers retain their action names (`compliance.seats_purchased.update`, `compliance.weekly_usage_limit.create/update`) and before/after/reason fields. Lost audit rows use the existing sanitized outcome, including whether persistence reached only the tamper-proof store or also the queryable chained record. No logger-only variable is used to suppress the discarded-outcome scanner.

The real Business Center guard remains stricter than a tenant administrator: current platform finance standing is required. The billing handlers retain their existing organization administrator/owner and reason-for-change checks. The licensing acceptance handler takes actor and organization from authenticated request context; supplied body identities cannot change them. Tests of the isolated router supply that context and do not establish JWT verification, native RLS or a change to who may bind an organization legally.

The actual call-chain search found no active client caller of the legacy Business Center CSV/rate endpoints, the seat/weekly configuration endpoints or `/api/licensing` agreement endpoints. These APIs are not being represented as a completed user-facing licensing workflow. The shared transport consumes canonical mutation outcomes, and the control tower owns the separate correction allowing explicit failed-audit headers from successful GET downloads to reach its global warning; ordinary GET response payloads stay unaffected.

Open adjacent legal work remains distinct: the baseline seeded agreements have no body or URL, the acceptance middleware is not mounted, that middleware expressly fails open, and agreement read failures currently degrade to an empty result. Supplying approved legal text, activating enforcement and determining organizational signing authority require their own established product/legal contract. None is inferred from this audit repair.
