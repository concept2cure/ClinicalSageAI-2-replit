### Behaviour, with the shipped flags, against a stand-in registry
CASE 1 - transient: 404 three times, then the real jar
   curl exit=0  checksum exit=0  elapsed=7s     <- recovers AND verifies
CASE 2 - tamper: a payload that is not the pinned artifact
   curl exit=0  checksum exit=1  elapsed=0s     <- integrity still refuses
CASE 3 - gone: 404 on every attempt
   curl exit=22 checksum exit=1  elapsed=255s   <- bounded give-up, build fails

# CASE 2 is the one that matters: retrying buys more chances at the PINNED
# bytes and nothing else. CASE 3 shows an absent artifact still stops the
# image, inside the 300s bound, rather than hanging or being skipped.

### Rejected first attempt, kept here because it is why the flags look as they do
--retry 5 --retry-delay 2 --retry-all-errors --retry-max-time 180
   against a sustained 429 from the real registry:
   curl: (22) The requested URL returned error: 429   (x6)
   RUN-body exit=22
# All five attempts burned in ~10s, inside the refusal window. A fixed delay
# defeats the point; curl's own exponential backoff does not.
