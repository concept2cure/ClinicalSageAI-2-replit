# Reporting companion selection — reader check

Status: manual browser/deployed-system check **not run**. The evidence README
records the separate automated mounted and server checks.

1. Open Reporting and a retained report bundle. Choose **Choose legal companions
   for ZIP**. No document should be checked automatically.
2. Use the legal-title search and document-kind filter, then **Next legal
   versions** / **Previous legal versions**. Each inventory request contains at
   most 25 records. Choices from another page/search remain selected and removable.
3. Select eligible exact versions. Expired, revoked, future or source-refused
   records show their reason and cannot be added. After 50 total choices, a further
   choice is disabled; removing a choice permits another selection.
4. Optionally choose published organization-wide stationery. Scoped designs
   remain unavailable without a verified target context. The selection applies
   only to this archive; it does not change the company's saved preference.
5. Download the reports and selected originals. Keep the ZIP and its manifest.
   The server checks current access, source identity and approval again. A refusal
   saves no archive. The archive does not determine agency acceptance or legal
   completeness. Existing PDF-only exports remain available separately.
6. Refresh the legal companions: choices clear and search starts again. A normal
   sign-in token refresh preserves current choices. Changing account or bundle
   withdraws the prior choices; a delayed prior download must not save a file.

This check covers the existing Reporting chooser. Company identity, logo,
letterhead design, legal registration, approval and submission placement remain
in their existing Settings/Submission workflows.
