Please refer to your Investigational New Drug Application (IND) submitted under section 505(i) of
the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for CBP-901 (ravotinib) capsules.
We also refer to your submission dated July 30, 2026 (Serial No. 0042), containing protocol
CBP-901-201 entitled "A Phase 2, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study of CBP-901 in
Patients with Moderate to Severe Plaque Psoriasis."
We have reviewed your submission and have the following comments and recommendations. We
request that you submit a protocol amendment addressing these comments before enrolling subjects.
Clinical
1. The proposed primary endpoint, the proportion of subjects achieving PASI 75 at Week 12, is
acceptable. However, we recommend that you also evaluate the proportion of subjects achieving an
sPGA score of 0 or 1 with at least a 2-grade improvement from baseline as a co-primary endpoint.
2. Your proposed exclusion criteria do not exclude subjects with a history of serious infections.
Revise the exclusion criteria to exclude subjects with active or chronic infections, including
tuberculosis, hepatitis B, and hepatitis C.
Biostatistics
3. Specify how intercurrent events, such as discontinuation of study drug due to lack of efficacy
or use of rescue medication, will be handled in the primary analysis. Refer to ICH E9(R1).
4. Provide the sample size calculation, including the assumed response rates, the type I error
rate, and the power.
Clinical Pharmacology
5. Collect sparse pharmacokinetic samples from all subjects at Weeks 2, 4, 8, and 12 to support a
population pharmacokinetic analysis.
Please submit your protocol amendment as a new submission to this IND. If you have any questions,
call Anne Walker, Regulatory Project Manager, at (301) 796-3333.
