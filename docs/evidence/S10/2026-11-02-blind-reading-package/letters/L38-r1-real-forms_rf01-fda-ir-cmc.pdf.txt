Please refer to your Investigational New Drug Application (IND) submitted under section 505(i)
of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for EXM-101 (examplinib) Tablets.
We also refer to your amendment dated September 12, 2026 (Serial No. 0007), containing
Chemistry, Manufacturing, and Controls (CMC) information.
We are reviewing the CMC section of your submission and have the following comments and
information requests. We request a written response by November 20, 2026, in order to continue
our evaluation of your IND.
Drug Substance
1. Provide the batch analysis data for all drug substance lots used in nonclinical toxicology
studies and the lot proposed for the clinical study. Include the results for residual solvents
and elemental impurities in accordance with ICH Q3C(R8) and ICH Q3D(R2).
2. Your proposed acceptance criterion for the unspecified impurities (NMT 0.20%) is not
acceptable. Revise the acceptance criterion to NMT 0.10% in accordance with ICH Q3A(R2), or
provide justification.
3. Provide a summary of the potential mutagenic impurities assessment per ICH M7(R2) for the
starting materials, intermediates, and reagents used in the synthesis. For each impurity

Drug Product
4. Provide the composition of the drug product, including the grade and function of each
excipient. Identify any excipients that are novel or of human or animal origin.
5. The proposed dissolution acceptance criterion of Q = 75% at 45 minutes is not adequately

report, including data demonstrating the discriminating ability of the method.
6. Provide the stability protocol for the clinical batches. The protocol should include the

a. the storage conditions and time points;
b. the tests and acceptance criteria; and
c. the container closure system.
7. Confirm that the drug product will be manufactured in accordance with current good
manufacturing practice (CGMP) for Phase 1 investigational drugs.
