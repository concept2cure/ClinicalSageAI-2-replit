We are reviewing your Section 510(k) premarket notification of intent to market the device
referenced above, and we have determined that additional information is necessary. To complete
the review of your submission, we request the following information:
1. Your device description does not identify all patient-contacting materials. Please provide a
complete list of the materials of construction for each patient-contacting component, including
any colorants, coatings, and adhesives, and identify the supplier for each material.
2. You have not provided adequate biocompatibility information for the balloon coating. Your
biocompatibility evaluation should follow the FDA guidance document "Use of International
Standard ISO 10993-1, Biological evaluation of medical devices - Part 1: Evaluation and testing
within a risk management process." Please provide the following:
