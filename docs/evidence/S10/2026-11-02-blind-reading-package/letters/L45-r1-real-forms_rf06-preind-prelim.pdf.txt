2.0 DISCUSSION
2.1. Chemistry, Manufacturing, and Controls
Question 1: Does the Agency agree that the proposed drug substance specification is adequate to
support the Phase 1 study?
FDA Response to Question 1: We generally agree. However, include a test for residual solvents
with acceptance criteria consistent with ICH Q3C(R8) and a test for bacterial endotoxins.
Question 2: Does the Agency agree that the proposed stability program for the drug product is
adequate to support the Phase 1 clinical study?
FDA Response to Question 2: Yes, your proposal appears reasonable. Provide the following in the
IND:
1. the stability protocol, including tests, acceptance criteria and time points;
2. the available stability data for the clinical batch.

2.2. Nonclinical
Question 3: Does the Agency agree that the completed GLP 28-day repeat-dose toxicology studies in
rats and dogs are adequate to support the proposed starting dose and dose escalation in the
first-in-human study?
FDA Response to Question 3: No. The rationale for the starting dose based on the HNSTD in dogs is
not adequate for a molecule with this mechanism. Provide a starting dose rationale based on the
STD10 in rats and one-sixth of the HNSTD in dogs, whichever is lower.
Question 4: Does the Agency agree that a separate embryo-fetal development study is not required
prior to initiation of the Phase 1 study in patients with advanced cancer?
FDA Response to Question 4: We agree, consistent with ICH S9.
3.0 OTHER IMPORTANT INFORMATION
PEDIATRIC PLANS
Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active
ingredients are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product
for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived or deferred.
