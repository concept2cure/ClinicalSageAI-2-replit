Please refer to your new drug application (NDA) dated December 18, 2025, received December 18,
2025, and your amendments, submitted pursuant to section 505(b)(1) of the Federal Food, Drug, and
Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Lumetrex (lumetinib) tablets, 25 mg and 50 mg.
We have completed our review of this application, as amended, and have determined that we cannot
approve this application in its present form. We have described our reasons for this action below
and, where possible, our recommendations to address these issues.
CLINICAL/STATISTICAL
1. Study LMX-301 did not demonstrate a statistically significant treatment effect on the primary
endpoint (change from baseline in FEV1 at Week 24). The estimated treatment difference was
42 mL (95% CI: -8, 92; p=0.10).
Recommendation: Conduct an additional adequate and well-controlled trial that demonstrates a
clinically meaningful and statistically significant effect on the primary endpoint.
2. The safety database is insufficient to characterize the risk of QT prolongation at the proposed
50 mg dose. Exposure-response analyses suggest a mean QTcF increase of 12 ms at the Cmax of the
50 mg dose.
Recommendation: Conduct a thorough QT study or provide concentration-QTc analyses from studies
with adequate exposure margins, consistent with ICH E14/S7B Q&As.

3. The proposed dissolution acceptance criterion (Q = 70% at 30 minutes) is not acceptable. The
data from the registration batches support a criterion of Q = 80% at 30 minutes. Revise the
acceptance criterion and submit the updated specification.
4. The stability data do not support the proposed 36-month expiration dating period. Based on the
submitted data, an expiration dating period of 24 months is supported.
FACILITY INSPECTIONS
5. During a recent inspection of the Meridian Pharma manufacturing facility (FEI 3001234567) for
this application, our field investigator conveyed deficiencies to the representative of the
facility. Satisfactory resolution of these deficiencies is required before this application may
be approved.

SAFETY UPDATE
When you respond to the above deficiencies, include a safety update as described at 21 CFR
314.50(d)(5)(vi)(b). The safety update should include data from all nonclinical and clinical
studies/trials of the drug under consideration regardless of indication, dosage form, or dose level.
OTHER
Within one year after the date of this letter, you are required to resubmit or take one of the
other actions available under 21 CFR 314.110. If you do not take one of these actions, we may
consider your lack of response a request to withdraw the application under 21 CFR 314.65.
A resubmission must fully address all the deficiencies listed in this letter and should be clearly
marked with "RESUBMISSION" in large font, bolded type at the beginning of the cover letter of the
submission.
The drug product may not be legally marketed until you have been notified in writing that this
application is approved.
