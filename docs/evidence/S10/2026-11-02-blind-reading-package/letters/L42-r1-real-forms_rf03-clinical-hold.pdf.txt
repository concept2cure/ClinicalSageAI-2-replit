We also refer to our telephone conversation on September 26, 2026, in which we informed you that
Protocol NVG-220-101, entitled "A Phase 1, First-in-Human, Single and Multiple Ascending Dose Study
of NVG-220 in Healthy Adult Subjects," is on clinical hold.
We have completed our review of your application, as amended, and have concluded that you may not
proceed with your proposed clinical investigation. Your IND is on clinical hold under 21 CFR
312.42(b)(1)(iv) because the IND does not contain sufficient information required under 21 CFR
312.23 to assess the risks to human subjects of the proposed studies. The specific deficiencies
and the information needed to resolve them are as follows:
CLINICAL HOLD DEFICIENCIES
1. The proposed starting dose of 3 mg/kg is not adequately supported. The no-observed-adverse-
effect level (NOAEL) in the 28-day repeat-dose toxicology study in cynomolgus monkeys was not
identified because adverse findings in the liver occurred at all dose levels.

To resolve this deficiency, provide a revised starting dose based on the minimum anticipated
biological effect level (MABEL), with the supporting pharmacology data and calculations.
2. Your protocol does not include adequate stopping rules for hepatotoxicity. Revise the protocol
to include individual and cohort stopping rules based on ALT, AST, and total bilirubin elevations,
consistent with the FDA guidance for industry Drug-Induced Liver Injury: Premarketing Clinical
Evaluation (July 2009).
3. The sentinel dosing plan is inadequate for a first-in-human study of a novel immunomodulator.
Revise the protocol so that, in each cohort, one subject receives NVG-220 and one subject receives
placebo, with a minimum observation period of 48 hours before the remaining subjects are dosed.
NON-HOLD COMMENTS
The following comments are not clinical hold issues; however, we recommend that you address
them in your complete response:
1. Include a schedule of assessments for anti-drug antibodies (ADA) in the protocol.
2. Provide the Investigator's Brochure updated with the 28-day toxicology study results.
Please identify your response to the clinical hold issues as a "CLINICAL HOLD COMPLETE RESPONSE."
To facilitate a timely review, also clearly label the cover letter and envelope. Following receipt
of your complete response, we will notify you within 30 days whether you may proceed with the
proposed clinical investigation. You may not initiate the clinical study until we notify you that
the study may proceed.
