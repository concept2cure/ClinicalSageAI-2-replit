# What backs the 2.5.6.1–2.5.6.5 heading rows (ich-m4-headings.ts, added 2026-10-20)

Filed 2026-10-25 at the 3a review's request. The first note on these rows claimed "a search extract of the table of
contents of the FDA-hosted copy". That claim was wrong, and the note now says what is below.

## What was attempted

- **WebFetch** `https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/M4E_R2__Guideline.pdf`: `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND database.ich.org`.
- **WebFetch** `https://www.hhs.gov/guidance/sites/default/files/hhs-guidance-documents/FDA/10326061fnl_M4ER2_The-CTD_Efficacy.pdf`:
  `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND www.hhs.gov`.
- **WebSearch** (one call, 2026-10-20). The query supplied five of the seven titles:
  `ICH M4E(R2) "2.5.6.1 Therapeutic Context" "2.5.6.1.1 Disease or Condition" "2.5.6.1.2 Current Therapies" "2.5.6.4 Benefit-Risk Assessment" "2.5.6.5 Appendix"`

## What the search returned

The search returned links and a model-written answer. It did not return an extract of the guideline. The answer,
verbatim:

> The search confirmed that the headings you listed are part of ICH M4E(R2)'s structure for section 2.5.6. The table
> of contents in the FDA-hosted copy of the guideline lists 2.5.6.1 Therapeutic Context with its subsections 2.5.6.1.1
> and 2.5.6.1.2, 2.5.6.4 Benefit-Risk Assessment, and 2.5.6.5 Appendix (the FDA-hosted copy of the guideline). That
> table also includes 2.5.6.2 Benefits and 2.5.6.3 Risks, which you didn't list.
>
> Other sources describe the same overall structure. The Federal Register notice says section 2.5.6 has four
> subsections: therapeutic context, benefit, risk, and benefit-risk assessment (Section 2.5.6 is divided into four
> subsections). […]
>
> I didn't find the text of the subsections or the appendix guidance in these results, so I can't confirm their
> detailed content. The official ICH guideline document would be the place to check that.

Links returned:
- [FDA-hosted M4E(R2), hhs.gov copy](https://www.hhs.gov/guidance/sites/default/files/hhs-guidance-documents/FDA/10326061fnl_M4ER2_The-CTD_Efficacy.pdf)
- [Federal Register notice, 2017-07-25](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2017-07-25/pdf/2017-15564.pdf)
- secondary sources: Huron, RAPS, FDAnews, Rho.

## What that supports

- **Numbering and wording.** The record's own 2.5.6 brief (`authoring-guidance.ts`) already named all seven headings,
  with this numbering and wording, before 2026-10-20. The search answer names the same seven. "Benefits" and "Risks"
  came from the answer itself, not from the query. That supports `recall`, not `regulator-text`.
- **The 2.5.6.5 condition** ("when detailed benefit-risk methods or results are presented"). It comes from the 2.5.6
  brief ("detailed benefit-risk methods/results when used"). It is not from the guideline's text, which was not read.
- **Owed.** Read the guideline itself when a regulator host resolves, then raise the basis.
