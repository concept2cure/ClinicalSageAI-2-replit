# LMN-201 RePreve/CDI02: proposed regulator-anticipation matrix

Prepared 27 October 2026. Target: **NCT05330182 / CDI02**.

## Purpose and evidence boundary

This is a proposed CSR-readiness checklist, not a record of agency feedback to Lumen, a finding of noncompliance or a verified review of LMN-201 patient data.

The highest-priority preparation is a defensible global-cure derivation, complete recurrence evidence, a reproducible prespecified Bayesian analysis, separate accounting for open-label and randomized cohorts, and exposure-based safety reporting. These are our proposed readiness checks. The comparator precedents below show why each question is useful; they are not questions known to have been sent by FDA or EMA to Lumen.

The existing public target profile records an estimated 375 participants and a global-cure window up to 16 weeks in the registry, while the 21 October sponsor release reports 45 participants (Part A 22; Part B 23) and a prespecified Bayesian SAP. Those descriptions must be reconciled against the final approved records, not resolved by assumption. The release is not a verified client database. Sources: [registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05330182), [sponsor release](https://www.lumen.bio/news/2026/10/22/lumen-bio-reports-positive-phase-2-results-from-the-re-preve-clinical-trial-lmn-201-for-c-difficile-infection), and the existing repository target profile recorded in the companion JSON.

## Concrete public-reporting reconciliation

Lumen’s own release reports Part B global cure of **10/12 versus 4/11** and an **odds ratio of 7.8**, while referring to its prespecified Bayesian analysis plan. A Lumen-hosted summary linking to Contagion gives **8.75**. The unadjusted cross-product odds ratio from those published counts is `(10 × 7) / (2 × 4) = 8.75`. [Primary sponsor release](https://www.lumen.bio/news/2026/10/22/lumen-bio-reports-positive-phase-2-results-from-the-re-preve-clinical-trial-lmn-201-for-c-difficile-infection); [secondary summary hosted by Lumen](https://www.lumen.bio/news/2026/10/22/lmn-201-reduces-c-difficile-recurrence).

**Our proposed check:** reconcile the estimator and effect label with the SAP, model, tables/listings/figures and code, including any adjustment, posterior summary, interval and success criterion. A model-based Bayesian estimate can differ from the crude ratio; whether that explains 7.8 remains unverified. This observation does not establish an error. These public counts have not been verified against client data, and this is not an agency question known to have been sent to Lumen.

## Indispensable client inputs

- **Authoritative design and analysis record:** Signed CDI02 protocol and every amendment; final and prior dated SAPs; approved estimand and success rule; database-cut, lock and unblinding chronology; relevant agency correspondence, if any.
- **Traceable clinical and derived data:** Randomization and actual treatment; disposition/deviations; initial cure and recurrence components; diagnostics, symptom diaries, medication/rescue timelines and follow-up; analysis-set flags and derivation specifications.
- **Bayesian implementation:** Actual model, priors, decision thresholds, simulations, interim history, executable analysis code and outputs. Establish whether Part A or external/historical data contribute; do not infer this from the press release.
- **Safety and product evidence:** Actual unique-person exposure by cohort and lot; AE/SAE/death narratives and safety windows; product-specific immunogenicity strategy; relevant potency, stability and comparability documentation.

Until those inputs are available, this package supports source-grounded planning and document review. It cannot validate the LMN-201 reported effect, approve an analysis set or establish that its CSR meets a regulator’s expectations.

### Review-history and status safeguards

The Zinplava statistical PDF contains both an original May 2016 review and a September 2016 addendum. The latter must accompany the original denominator critique; the earlier reviewer’s position is not a standalone final agency rejection. [Original discussion, PDF62](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2016/761046Orig1s000StatR.pdf#page=62); [later addendum, PDF13](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2016/761046Orig1s000StatR.pdf#page=13).

EMA’s live Zinplava page states that the EU authorisation was withdrawn on **20 October 2026 at the holder’s request for commercial reasons**. The historical assessment is useful as a review precedent; it is not evidence of a currently authorised EU product or a safety-driven withdrawal. The downloaded original retains EMA’s current status watermark. [EMA status page](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/zinplava).

The retained ICH documents are the E3 final guideline dated 30 November 1995 and E9(R1) final addendum dated 20 November 2019. The [official ICH index](https://www.ich.org/page/search-index-ich-guidelines) and [EMA’s E3 guideline page](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/ich-e3-structure-content-clinical-study-reports-scientific-guideline) identify the adopted versions; local implementation and study-specific agreements still govern application.

## Verification, attribution and use

All seven original files passed PDF page-count inspection, were rehashed, and were checked against the previously retained source hashes. Native extraction produced the same number of page segments as each PDF. Ten key original pages were rendered and visually checked for the cited passages. The receipt distinguishes that limited review from qualification of whole documents, tables or OCR output. Rebyota’s statistical page 21 contains a table whose text order is unreliable; the narrative evidence and original page were checked. Zinplava medical page 113 uses an OCR derivative and was visually checked.

Sources are acknowledged as the **U.S. Food and Drug Administration**, **European Medicines Agency** and **International Council for Harmonisation**. Original bytes are unmodified; the checklist is our synthesis and implies no agency endorsement. FDA’s [website policy](https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/about-website/website-policies), EMA’s [legal notice](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/about-us/about-website/legal-notice) and the ICH document notices provide the recorded reuse context, including attribution and third-party exclusions. This research package does not approve model training or substitute for document-level rights review before a new application use.

No database import, application admission, deployment or model training was performed. No agency correspondence to Lumen was supplied or inferred.
