# AnA CSR exemplar review — NIH/BPCA Meropenem

**Status: document inspection, NOT Cortex production admission, training permission or clinical verification.**

Source: retained original `NIH_NBK588444_nichdmeropen_CSR.pdf` in the prior LMN-201 reference acquisition archive. Reviewed the local 453-page original, including selected front matter, report body and appendix tables. This is a composite PDF; the actual CSR begins at physical PDF page 28 (printed CSR page 1), not physical page 1. Pages 1–27 include other material, and later pages contain tabulations. **Do not assume physical PDF page equals CSR printed page or treat every page as report narrative.**

Observed exemplar features (page numbers refer to physical PDF pages, 1-indexed):
- p.28: title page identifies a multiple-dose meropenem PK study in young infants and the protocol. The CSR carries a printed page number separate from the PDF page.
- p.36: structured sponsor/product information and methodology in the synopsis portion.
- p.46: investigators and administrative structure, including multiple sites.
- p.56: investigational-product identity and formulation/packaging information.
- p.69: AE incidence reporting by gestational/postnatal age strata and serious outcomes.
- p.82: exposure/target attainment modeling and PK interpretation; **model-derived predictions must not be rewritten as directly observed participant outcomes**.
- p.96: vital-sign and safety observations.
- p.121 onward: separately formatted statistical tables with data-closure and report dates; source table numbers are distinct from narrative section numbers.

Specific AnA learning objectives:
1. Distinguish sponsor CSR body, front-matter label material and TLF appendix before indexing and quoting.
2. Preserve physical-page/printed-page/table-number mapping and source version.
3. Match model predictions to the method and population; do not equate simulations with observed efficacy.
4. Keep subgroup denominators, safety populations, AE definitions, units and footnotes intact.
5. Reconcile the narrative's summary values to actual appendix TLFs; flag mismatches instead of rewriting them silently.
6. Distinguish drug-specific pediatric PK conventions from LMN-201's recurrent-CDI study: **transfer reporting craft, never patient findings or efficacy claims**.

Limitations: This review does not exhaustively inspect all 453 pages, verify all numerical claims, or establish legal permission for commercial embedding/training. The original and derivatives remain retained. Do not mark this document as a qualified production source without source-level rights and integrity checks.
