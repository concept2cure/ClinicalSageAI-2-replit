Afterwards: the rule refusing a leaf filed directly in a heading that has
sub-headings (3, 3.2, 5.3) was withdrawn. The ICH DTD lets every heading hold
leaves, so such a leaf can be in a validly filed sequence, and refusing it would
keep that whole sequence out of the filed state. Its mutation is no longer
listed here. A second, independent verifier raised it, and the design note
records the decision. Those leaves are now read as filed, and a test pins it.
