## Reading
With the binder digesting the commitment without the Vault sha256, the binder unit tests fail on the shared recipe and on "a different pin is a different digest". In the real-PostgreSQL run the fulfilment still signs (the ceremony accepts whatever digest the binder gives), but the signature's bound_payload_digest is no longer the recipe's digest over the record ("signs: ... and the signature binds them" fails on that comparison), and the list, which recomputes the recipe through the same reader and compares, no longer reports the signature as binding the record ("the list names ... that the signature matches the record" fails). The other 16 cases in the selection pass. The sha256 is part of what is signed, and a binder that drops it is caught twice.
