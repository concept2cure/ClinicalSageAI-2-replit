## Reading
Without the read-only pre-check, evidence the server refuses is discovered only inside the signing transaction, after the password is checked. In the route test the credential check is now called for a refused request, and the evidence is resolved once instead of twice. In the real-PostgreSQL run each refused case (sent deliberately with a wrong password, as the test does to prove the password is never reached) spends one of the signer's attempts: the first five refusals answer 401 PASSWORD_VERIFICATION_FAILED instead of the evidence's own 409 or 404, and from the sixth the answer is 423 ACCOUNT_LOCKED (§11.300). A person naming the wrong sequence would lock their own account. That is the harm the pre-check removes. Control: "M10 unit" 10 passed, "M10 db" 11 passed.
