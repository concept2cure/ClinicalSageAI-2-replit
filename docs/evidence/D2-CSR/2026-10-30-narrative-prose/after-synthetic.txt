===== SYN-NARR-0005 [ 'aesi' ] 
Subject SYN-NARR-0005, a 34-year-old male subject randomized to the LMN-201 arm in study SYN-NARR. Relevant medical history included Type 2 diabetes mellitus. Concomitant medications included PARACETAMOL. The subject received LMN-201 beginning 2026-01-09. On study day 15, the subject experienced an adverse event of Alanine aminotransferase increased, mild in intensity. The dose of LMN-201 was not changed. The event was not serious. The event resolved. The investigator assessed the event as possibly related to LMN-201.

The subject received LMN-201 for 28 days.
===== SYN-NARR-0004 [ 'serious' ] 
Subject SYN-NARR-0004, a 33-year-old female subject randomized to the Placebo arm in study SYN-NARR. Relevant medical history included Hypertension. Concomitant medications included PARACETAMOL. The subject received Placebo beginning 2026-01-08. On study day 10, the subject experienced a serious adverse event of Pneumonia, moderate in intensity. The event was serious: it required inpatient hospitalization or prolongation of existing hospitalization. The dose of Placebo was not changed. The event resolved. The investigator assessed the event as not related to Placebo.

The subject received Placebo for 28 days.
===== SYN-NARR-0008 [ 'death', 'serious', 'discontinuation' ] 
Subject SYN-NARR-0008, a 37-year-old male subject randomized to the Placebo arm in study SYN-NARR. Relevant medical history included Hypertension. Concomitant medications included PARACETAMOL. The subject received Placebo beginning 2026-01-12. On study day 19, the subject experienced a serious adverse event of Septic shock, severe in intensity. The event was serious: it resulted in death. Placebo was permanently discontinued. The event was fatal. The investigator assessed the event as not related to Placebo.

The subject received Placebo for 19 days. The subject died on study day 21. The recorded cause of death was septic shock.
