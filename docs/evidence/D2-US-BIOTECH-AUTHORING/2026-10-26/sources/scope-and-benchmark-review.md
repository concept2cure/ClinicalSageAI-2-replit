# US biotech scope and current public authoring benchmark

Read-only review, 2026-10-26. This is a requirements review for human therapeutic biotechnology, biologics, vaccines, biosimilars and relevant combination products. It is not an exhaustive FDA legal universe, a second filing registry, a regulatory applicability determination or a parity verdict.

## What already exists

The canonical registry supplies32US medicinal and17GLOBAL medicinal rows in the discovery snapshot. Source outlines, FDA lifecycle records, the sole editor, source import/assembly, AnA drafting/remedies, review/signatures, SPL and E2B engines and authority-response services already exist. Their presence is credited. An absent exact editor start is not an absent backend service. The current increment reaches twelve detailed lifecycle records and completes clinical metadata, with separate executable qualification owned by the implementation workers.

`us-biotech-scope-requirements.json` groups22 regulatory/document needs and names existing registry IDs, owners, observed limits and acceptance evidence. `weave-public-requirements.json` groups nine public benchmark requirements. These are grouped requirements, not extra filing-denominator rows or verified implementation labels.

## Current source findings that matter to implementation

The official FDA electronic-safety page states that postmarketing ICSRs sent through ESGNextGen use E2B(R3) from October1,2026. It separately describes the April1,2026 commercial-IND electronic reporting condition and identifies SRP and IND-exempt BA/BE distinctions. These observations do not mean every safety narrative must be sent through the same route. Source/version, report subtype and sponsor/channel facts must control the actual workflow.

The current eCFR pages for Parts600/601 were readable, including the separate report headings above. The Part601 page also links an amendment; effective versions and application-specific obligations must be checked before changing production rules or calculating deadlines. This review does not certify every linked guidance or regulator paragraph as current for a future filing.

The initial FDA application-types and RFD URLs in the root source index returned404; those failures remain recorded. The RFD guidance was recovered from FDA's jurisdiction page and successfully read at `https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/how-write-request-designation-rfd`. The old individual-patient expanded-access link also returned404; the official Form3926 guidance and group-treatment page were successfully retrieved instead. A failed URL is not evidence that a regulatory mechanism is absent.

Security/privacy statements are also vendor claims, not Concept2Cure evidence. The page does not substantiate head-to-head time savings, accuracy, regulatory correctness, full template depth, exact citation granularity, formatting fidelity or direct agency-transmission comparisons.

## Practical acceptance boundary

The whole journey needs evidence: recorded project/filing/product facts → admitted source versions → exact working document → manual/AnA drafting and tracked revision → source review and human approval → faithful document/export → applicable validated package or reporting channel. One successful stage does not establish the later stages.

The32-row medicinal count omits cross-segment EUA/RFD and cannot prove all plausible biotech filings. Veterinary, stand-alone device/IVD, HCT/P361, controlled-substance, state, laboratory, institutional and other specialty obligations are not exhaustively assessed here. Relevant existing owners must be traced before adding new templates. No row in this review is marked VERIFIED, no tests or Git/global commands were run, and no provider or agency write occurred.

Source URLs, successful/failed retrievals and SHA256 are retained in `public-source-index.json` and `lifecycle-public-source-index.json`. Raw HTML remains temporary research input; these retained summaries are authored paraphrases.
