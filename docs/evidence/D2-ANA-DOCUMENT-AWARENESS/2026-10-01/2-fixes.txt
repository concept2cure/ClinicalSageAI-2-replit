## What changed (by review finding)

1  Reversible redlines. Drafted blocks never split a paragraph: they go between
   blocks (after the paragraph; before it when a caret is at its start), and
   their text carries `ownsBlock` (data-owns-block on <ins>/<del>), so the
   decision that removes the text removes the paragraph it made (rejecting a
   drafted paragraph) or wholly replaced (accepting the strike of a paragraph
   the quote/selection covered entirely) — checked at decision time
   (deleteDecided: whole content, every node owned, container can lose it).
   A replacement continuing its paragraph (quote/selection ending it, or a
   caret in an empty paragraph) puts its first paragraph inline, unowned.
   proposeReplacement refuses `structural` for blocks after a quote that ends
   mid-sentence; insert-over-selection puts them after that paragraph; a caret
   mid-sentence puts them after the paragraph. Contract tests: reject-all ==
   original HTML exactly (live, and after reload from saved HTML), accept-all
   == intended HTML, for proposeReplacement, insert over a selection and the
   caret insert.
2  insert-over-selection: the struck selection is the person's (storage.author,
   no turn record); the insertion stays AnA's.
3  Inline replacements/insertions keep their leading/trailing whitespace
   (only blank lines at the edges are dropped).
4  EditorBridgeProposal requires sectionId (wrong-section otherwise); takes
   baseSha256 (hex SHA-256 of the STORED section content as loaded, via
   crypto.subtle — the value authoring-read computes in SQL) and/or
   baseUpdatedAt; neither -> no-base; either differing -> stale; a bridge whose
   effect was cleaned up (save, revert, section switch) -> stale, checked again
   after the async hash. propose returns a Promise.
5  Quote matching: every whitespace run, NBSP included, is one space, and the
   match strikes the whole run it read; prefix/suffix drop the reader's
   line-start markers (#, -, *, +, N., N), "Table:") and read " | " as
   whitespace on both sides. A quote across a paragraph/cell break stays
   not-found; the ProposeRefusal doc says so.
6  Occurrences touching a pending suggestion are not counted toward ambiguity;
   if they are the only ones -> overlaps-suggestion.
8  proposeReplacement no longer sets the selection or scrolls; the person's
   selection is mapped through the change.
9  Given prefix/suffix must fit: a single occurrence they contradict, or none
   of several -> not-found (the old "context fitting none of several" case was
   'ambiguous'; that test now pins not-found, and 'ambiguous' is pinned with
   context both occurrences carry).
10 Tests added: quote running into a pending <del>, the sealed branch isolated
   (RichSectionEditor stubbed always-editable; the control reaches it, the
   sealed document does not), trailing/leading whitespace, structural reject
   round-trips, bridge after a real Save (old bridge and new bridge refuse the
   pre-save hash and updated_at; the post-save bases are taken).
   conversationThreadCanvas.test.tsx: jsdom rect polyfill only. (Not
   reproducible red at this point: with proposeReplacement no longer scrolling
   the file ran clean before the polyfill too — 18/18, no unhandled error.)

## Not fixed, and why

- A draft that ENDS the section with a table/list gets StarterKit TrailingNode's
  empty paragraph after it; that paragraph carries no mark and survives a
  rejection. Indistinguishable from an empty last paragraph the author kept, so
  removing it would be the wrong guess half the time. Documented in
  suggestions.ts ("KNOWN LIMIT"); the contract tests place blocks before a
  following paragraph.
- Accepting the strike of a whole paragraph is only paragraph-removing for the
  proposal/selection doors (ownsBlock); a person's own hand strike of a whole
  paragraph still leaves the empty paragraph on accept, as it always has.
- Hard breaks: collectSuggestions counts text nodes only, so a struck <br>
  inside a selection is left behind on accept (pre-existing; probe S8). Quotes
  never match across a hard break.
- Prefix/suffix that run through a pending suggestion do not fit (the reader
  labels suggestions; the editor's text does not) and are refused not-found
  rather than re-rendered in the reader's label format, which lives in the
  server module and would be a second copy of it.
