# SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION — Protocol FBT-201-2026 v2.0

Sponsor: Faux Biotech Therapeutics (fictional). Investigational product: FBT-201, an invented injectable investigational biologic. This is not human-subject research.

## Objective
Estimate the difference in PASI 75 response at Week 12 between FBT-201 200 mg and placebo in adults with moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis.

## Design
Phase 2, parallel-group, randomized 1:1, double-blind, placebo-controlled, simulated N=160 (80 per arm). Baseline and Week 12 PASI assessments are illustrative.

## Primary endpoint
PASI 75 at Week 12, binary responder (Y/N), ITT analysis population comprising all randomized fictional subject IDs.

## Safety
Treatment-emergent adverse event (TEAE), serious adverse event (SAE), adverse-event withdrawal, and a simulated ALT chemistry panel. Exposure and dose-administration source data are not provided and must not be invented in the CSR.

## Limitations
Every source record is algorithmically synthetic. No ethics approval, patient consent, real trial registration, clinical efficacy or safety inference is asserted.
