# Regulatory Build Ledger (RBL)

## What it is
A reproducible-build manifest for regulatory submissions:
- Inputs (evidence) with hashes/provenance
- Derived artifacts with hashes
- Generator version (commit/build)
- Claim/standard/outcome/section traceability map
- Approvals at export time
- Diff vs previous export

## Why it matters
- Audit-ready in minutes
- Defensibility for every claim
- Reduced review risk and rework

## Deliverables
- build_ledger.json (machine)
- build_ledger_report.pdf (human)
- defensibility_pack.zip (bundle)
