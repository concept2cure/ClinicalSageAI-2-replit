Session `…01E8btkB`. Asked by the founder on 2026-10-23: offer everything
Weave does, and what Veeva Basics gives a biotech, and more. This re-measures
Weave against today's code. **Veeva Basics is already measured** in

## What Weave offers (vendor and press sources, 2026-10-23)

- **AutoIND:** drafts IND sections from uploaded source study reports. The
  published Takeda benchmark drafted the nonclinical written summaries
  (2.6.2, 2.6.4, 2.6.6) in about 3 hours against about 100 manually. This is a
  vendor-linked study (arXiv 2509.09738).
- **Traceability:** each generated sentence and table maps to its source
  paragraph, for the reviewer to verify.
- **HAQ / RTQ:** health-authority questions taken from the letter, tracked,
  with responses drafted from prior submissions (expanded with Takeda in 2026).
- **AutoCT:** clinical documents: protocols, safety reports.
- **Coverage claim:** Modules 2–5, annual reports, safety updates, responses
  to regulators. This comes from a secondary source, not the vendor.

The fetches of weave.bio and veeva.com failed on DNS from this container, so
these come from search results that quote the vendors and their partners.

### 2. Sentence-level source traceability: partial (launch)

Exists: `document_span_lineage` (character ranges, source checksum at
citing, provenance kind), `span-lineage.service.ts`, `source-attribution.ts`,
`machine-attribution.ts`, `drafting-source-lineage.ts` for AnA text, the
figure checks `draft-figure-check.ts` and `section-claims-check.ts`,
`authoring_citations`, and `/api/data-origins` with `DataOriginsPanel.tsx` and
`DocumentAttributionBar.tsx` in the editor.

Gap: paraphrase is deliberately not attributed, since only verbatim clauses
are "quoted". There is no reviewer queue that verifies each citation in turn.
`sentenceTraceabilityService.ts` is reachable only through `/api/audit-services`,
which is not served. **The work:** complete a per-citation verification step
inside the launch Review surface, over the span lineage that exists. Whether
derived sentences may carry an attribution is a design question, because the
current rule refuses to claim a source for words that are not the source's.

### 3. HAQ intake and response: partial (launch)

Exists: `server/routes/c2c/agency-responses.ts` with
`services/agency-responses/*`. It holds the questions, a response per
question, the Part 11 signed approval, commitments and the response package
DOCX. The UI is `AgencyInbox.tsx`, `AgencyResponses.tsx` and `ProjectRespond.tsx`,
reached from communication-center and project-home. A letter parser exists
(`services/regulatory-correspondence/issue-parser.ts`,
`POST /api/regulatory-correspondence/correspondence/intake`, launch-claimed).

Gap:
- Questions are typed in by hand, and no client calls the parser. **Reach it.**
- No drafted response inside the response record. `draft_fda_ir_response`
  returns format instructions and writes nothing. **Complete it**, from the
  program's own record, with lineage.
- The registry names tools that do not exist (`draft_haq_response`,
  `search_precedent_responses`, `draft_agency_response`, `plan_crl_response`).
  **Fix the registry.**

## Decisions for the founder (Rule 2: widening the catalog is not a session's)

1. **Admit the nonclinical study-report path** (`/api/preclinical` ingest and
   the recorded loader) to the launch catalog, under Authoring. Weave's core
   offer depends on it, and it is built and off. It needs the two flags on in
   the deploy, a launch surface to upload from, and the 2.6/2.4 fix above.
   Without it, matching AutoIND is not possible honestly.
2. **The clinical and safety documents:** IB, IND safety reports, DSUR.
   Built in part, outside the catalog.
3. **The Veeva Basics areas `VEEVA_GAP` places outside the catalog,** with
   what exists:
   - **eTMF:** built (`Etmf.tsx`, `/api/etmf`, DIA reference model, inspection
     package), locked in production. No TMF QC/approval UI.
   - **CTMS:** studies, sites, enrollment, monitoring visits, deviations and
     milestones (`/api/clinical-operations`, `clinical_ops.*`), unmapped and so
     refused. Site communication tracking and issue management are absent.
   - **QMS CAPA and deviations:** routes and tables exist
     (`protocol-deviations.ts`, `protocol_capa_actions`). The Quality app has
     only SOP and Change tabs. Suppliers, audits, management review and
     nonconformance are API-only, and the first four are excluded at launch
     (`LAUNCH_EXCLUDED_API`).
   - **Training assignments and a role matrix:** absent; read-and-understood
     records exist. `VEEVA_GAP` §5 covers this.
