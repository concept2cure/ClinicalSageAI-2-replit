# What a biotech gets from Veeva that it does not yet get here — re-measured 2026-10-22

Founder direction, 2026-10-22: *"Continue to close the gap between what we do and what biotechs currently get from Veeva."*

This re-measures, at `9e77eca23`, the three Veeva categories that sit inside the launch catalog:

- RIM and eCTD submissions (Vault RIM: Submissions, Publishing, Archive, Registrations);
- regulated document management (Vault DMS);
- controlled documents (QualityDocs).

The lens is the one from July (`docs/audit-2026-07/13-competitive-scorecard.md`): what ships and is reachable in the product, traced from a rail entry to a mounted route. Three read-only audits ran, one per category. Their full tables, with `file:line` for every score, are summarised here; findings worth acting on are named with their evidence.

## Where July's verdict no longer holds

- **Vault is a real repository and a launch app** (July: repository 1, reachability 1; now 4 and 5). It stores, versions and re-verifies bytes against their recorded SHA-256. Signatures are bound to the version, history is chained per document, and a Vault version becomes an eCTD leaf by its own bytes.
- **Submission Center is a rail destination with a signed publish path** (July: publishing workflow 1; now 4). The path runs freeze, dispatch, transmit and package download. AS2 uses real CMS signing and encryption with pinned-MDN verification. Lifecycle operations are derived across sequences.
- **Quality is on the rail with signed writes** (July: reachability 1; now 4). Approvals and retirements are Part 11 signatures with no self-approval. Sample rows are gone.
- **Health-authority correspondence is strong** (not scored in July; now 4).
  - Letters carry due dates.
  - Questions carry owners, and responses carry signed approval.
  - Commitments are fulfilled by a signature naming the sequence or Vault version.

Outside product work, and in front of every regulated buyer: the validation package (VMP, URS, OQ, IQ, VSR) exists but every document is DRAFT, UNSIGNED and not executed at this version (`docs/validation/VSR-001…`). Veeva sells a validated cloud.

Outside the launch catalog, so not ranked:
