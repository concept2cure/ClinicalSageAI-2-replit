# What a biotech gets from Veeva that it does not yet get here: re-measured 2026-10-22

Founder direction, 2026-10-22: *"Continue to close the gap between what we do and what biotechs currently get from Veeva."*

This re-measures, at `9e77eca23`, the three Veeva categories that sit inside the launch catalog:

- RIM and eCTD submissions (Vault RIM: Submissions, Publishing, Archive, Registrations);
- regulated document management (Vault DMS);
- controlled documents (QualityDocs).

The lens is the one from July (`docs/audit-2026-07/13-competitive-scorecard.md`): what ships and is reachable in the product, traced from a rail entry to a mounted route.

**Revised the same day under CLAUDE.md Rule 3.** The first version of this document named gaps without establishing what already exists. Four searches then ran, each by every name, through code that nothing reaches, and through `git log --all --diff-filter=D`. They found that almost every gap is a completion, a reach or a fix of something that exists. That includes an in-browser viewer that already ships, and two per-section stores carrying owner and status fields. Each gap below carries its **What already exists** section and names its work accordingly. A design for any of them starts from that section, not from this summary.

## Where July's verdict no longer holds

- **Vault is a real repository and a launch app** (July: repository 1, reachability 1; now 4 and 5). It stores, versions and re-verifies bytes against their recorded SHA-256. Signatures are bound to the version, history is chained per document, and a Vault version becomes an eCTD leaf by its own bytes.
- **Submission Center is a rail destination with a signed publish path** (July: publishing workflow 1; now 4). The path runs freeze, dispatch, transmit and package download. AS2 uses real CMS signing and encryption with pinned-MDN verification. Lifecycle operations are derived across sequences.
- **Quality is on the rail with signed writes** (July: reachability 1; now 4).
- **Health-authority correspondence is strong** (not scored in July; now 4).
  - Letters carry due dates.
  - Questions carry owners, and responses carry signed approval.
  - Commitments are fulfilled by a signature naming the sequence or Vault version.

## 2. Content plan: complete it on the stores that exist. Do not add a third

**Veeva gives:** planned documents per CTD section, each with owner, due date and status, linked to the document that fills it.

**What already exists**

- **`c2c_document_sections`**: the persisted planned-section store every launch program gets.
  - Writer: `scaffold-project-documents.ts:173`, called from the New project wizard through `POST /api/c2c/projects`. It writes one row per rule-pack section, with status `todo`, the mandatory flag and an `owner_id` column.
  - Readers: the Authoring editor's outline (`GET /api/c2c/documents/:id/outline`, `useFilingOutline.ts`); the Vault CTD spine, which shows status, percent and owner (`project-vault.ts:670-686`); Project home workstreams and readiness.
  - **Nothing writes `owner_id`**: `PATCH /:id/sections/:key` takes content, status and reason only (`documents.ts:407-433`). There is no due-date column.
- **`project_sections`** (`db/migrations/20260220_ind_section_tracking.sql`).
  - Fields: owner, reviewer, deadline, 9-state status, status log, comments and dependencies.
  - It has a full API (`server/routes/project-sections.ts`: assign, deadline, status, history), mounted and claimed by the launch `tasks` surface.
  - **No live writer.** The launch wizard does not seed it, and it sits on the integer `projects.id` spine.
  - **It has a client reader again** (corrected 2026-10-29): since `8b192f1e7` (2026-10-26, client-journey slice A2.7) Author's `ProjectDossier` (`ModuleCompletion`) reads it through the dossier map assembler as `supportingReadiness.sectionTracking`, kept apart from governed sections and artifact status. Every launch project still reads it empty.
  - `register-document-routes.ts:236-245` names it the canonical home of section status, owner and deadline, and says not to rebuild it in parallel.
- **Required deliverables** (`SubmissionRequiredDeliverables.tsx`, `GET /api/submissions/sequences/:seqId/pathway-manifest`).
  - It shows per-sequence required/optional slots with Present/Missing status and the source that fills each, and opens the source in the editor.
  - It is computed, with nothing persisted.
  - **It is unavailable for drug eCTD pathways (IND, NDA, BLA, MAA)**: `ENGINE_BY_REGISTRY` maps only non-eCTD pathways (`submission-request-read.ts:170-196`).
- **`resolveRequiredSections`** (`server/services/ectd/required-sections.ts:189`): the drug eCTD required list, already used by Vault coverage (VR-15) and the compile status.
- **`unified_tasks`**: assignee, due date, status and a free `source_entity_type`/`source_entity_id`. It has an unused `deliverable_id` column (`shared/schema.ts:7618`). The assign-with-due-date pattern is `AssignReviewDialog.tsx:182-234`.
- **Read-only planners that persist nothing**:
  - the Planner tab (Module 1 only);
  - `plan_submission` (AnA, with `POST /api/submissions/:id/plan`, which has no client caller);
  - `build_pathway_manifest`.
- **`authoring_plans`** (`db/migrations/20260508_authoring_plans.sql`, `/api/ana/authoring-plan*`). Not on `C2C_MIGRATION_FILES`, so the table is absent where deployed. No client caller.
- **Deleted**:
  - `server/routes/content-plan.js` and its `content_plan_sections` tables (on no applier) in `35785b4cb`;
  - `ContentPlan.jsx` and `ContentPlanDialog.jsx` in `7a144fd1e`;
  - `EctdPlanner.jsx` in `b49e7495f`.
  - Unrendered `.cp-row` / `.cp-owner` styles remain in `surfaces-v2.css:169-200`.

**The work**

1. Complete owner and due date on the planned section (`c2c_document_sections`), either by giving `owner_id` a writer or through a `unified_tasks` row pointing at the section row.
2. Reach it from Required deliverables and Vault coverage.
3. Extend Required deliverables to drug eCTD through `resolveRequiredSections`.
4. Converge or retire `project_sections` and the caller-less `authoring_plans` in the same change.

Which store is canonical for section ownership needs a decision before code. `register-document-routes.ts` names `project_sections`, but every launch program writes `c2c_document_sections`. The client-journey lane (`.design/client-journey/`, slices A2 onward) now reads `project_sections` as Author's section tracking, so the decision is taken with that lane, not by a session alone (2026-10-29).

## 3. In-browser viewer: reach the one that ships. Renditions: reach the engine. Page annotations: absent

**What already exists**

## 4. Assigned review and approval routing: converge Vault and QMS onto the review loop that exists

**Veeva gives:** named reviewers and approvers, multi-signer routes, due dates, tasks, reminders and an inbox.

**What already exists**

- **The Authoring review loop is the one complete named-reviewer path.** It runs:
  1. `SendForReviewDialog.tsx`;
  2. `POST /api/authoring/documents/:id/request-review`, which writes one `authoring_reviews` row and one Reviewer grant per reviewer;
  3. a task per reviewer with assignee and due date;
  4. `notifyTaskEvent`;
  5. `taskDueSweep` (due-soon and overdue);
  6. `TaskTray` and Review's "Awaiting my review".

  The verdict closes the task.
- **A multi-signer chain**: `authoring_workflow_steps`, created by `POST /api/authoring/docs/:docId/submit` (`authoring.router.ts:7129`) and advanced by e-sign. **No client caller.**
- **`unified_tasks.approvers`**: enforced by `task-signoff.ts:130-181`, which has no writer.
- **Vault lifecycle** (`VaultLifecycle.tsx`, `document-lifecycle.ts`): one review and one approval Part 11 signature with separation of duties. Eligible signers are listed by name ("Not assigned to one person"). It sets no assignee, due date, task or notification.
- **QMS** (`qmsApproval.ts`, `controlled-document-service.ts:142`, `document-approval-signature.ts`):
  - send for review is a status change;
  - approval is one signature by any signing-authority role;
  - nothing emits a notification, although `notification-record-target.ts` already resolves a `qms_document` target.
- **Duplicate review stores, all outside Vault and QMS, to converge onto rather than extend**:
  - Phase-13 review threads and tasks (`server/routes/c2c/reviews.ts`; creation has no client caller);
  - P12 artifact reviewers (`artifacts.ts:3513-3965`; no client caller);
  - the unified workflow store (`ApprovalOrchestrator.ts`, `/api/approval-workflows`; MDX only);

- **Deleted**:

  - `ReviewerAssignment.tsx` and `SignatureWorkflow.tsx` in `e5a74a4df`;
  - the review-board write routes in `b4eb62419`;

**The work**
