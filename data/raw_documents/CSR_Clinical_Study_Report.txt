CLINICAL STUDY REPORT

Study Title: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Phase III Study of OncoMab in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors

Protocol Number: ONC-P3-2025-001
Study Drug: OncoMab (Investigational Monoclonal Antibody)
Sponsor: Global Pharma Inc.
Report Date: December 15, 2024

1.0 SYNOPSIS

1.1 Study Objectives
Primary Objective: To evaluate the efficacy of OncoMab compared to placebo in terms of overall survival in patients with advanced solid tumors.

Secondary Objectives:
- Assess progression-free survival
- Evaluate objective response rate
- Assess safety and tolerability
- Characterize pharmacokinetic profile

1.2 Study Design
This was a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase III study conducted at 25 sites across the United States and Europe.

2.0 STUDY POPULATION

2.1 Inclusion Criteria
- Adults ≥18 years with histologically confirmed advanced solid tumors
- ECOG performance status 0-2
- Adequate organ function
- Life expectancy ≥3 months

2.2 Exclusion Criteria
- Prior exposure to anti-PD-1/PD-L1 therapy
- Active autoimmune disease
- Pregnancy or nursing

3.0 STUDY RESULTS

3.1 Patient Disposition
- Screened: 512 patients
- Randomized: 450 patients
- Completed study: 387 patients
- Discontinued early: 63 patients

3.2 Efficacy Results
Primary Endpoint - Overall Survival:
- Median OS OncoMab arm: 14.2 months (95% CI: 12.1-16.8)
- Median OS Placebo arm: 11.3 months (95% CI: 9.8-12.9)
- Hazard Ratio: 0.73 (95% CI: 0.61-0.88), p=0.0012

Secondary Endpoints:
- Median PFS OncoMab: 6.1 months vs Placebo: 4.2 months (HR=0.68, p<0.001)
- ORR OncoMab: 23% vs Placebo: 12% (p=0.008)

4.0 SAFETY RESULTS

4.1 Adverse Events
Treatment-emergent adverse events occurred in 95% of patients in the OncoMab arm and 89% in the placebo arm.

Most common AEs (≥10% incidence):
- Fatigue: OncoMab 45%, Placebo 32%
- Nausea: OncoMab 38%, Placebo 25%
- Diarrhea: OncoMab 28%, Placebo 18%
- Rash: OncoMab 22%, Placebo 8%

4.2 Serious Adverse Events
SAEs were reported in 28% of OncoMab patients vs 22% of placebo patients. No treatment-related deaths occurred.

5.0 DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSIONS

5.1 Efficacy
OncoMab demonstrated statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement in overall survival compared to placebo.

5.2 Safety
The safety profile was consistent with the known toxicity profile of the drug class. No new safety signals were identified.

5.3 Benefit-Risk Assessment
The benefit-risk profile supports the use of OncoMab in the studied patient population.

6.0 STUDY LIMITATIONS
- Open-label extension phase results not yet mature
- Biomarker analysis ongoing

7.0 REGULATORY IMPLICATIONS
Results support filing of a New Drug Application (NDA) with FDA and Marketing Authorization Application (MAA) with EMA.
