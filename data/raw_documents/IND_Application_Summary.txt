INVESTIGATIONAL NEW DRUG APPLICATION

IND Number: 123456
Drug Name: OncoMab (Anti-PD-L1 Monoclonal Antibody)
Sponsor: Global Pharma Inc.
Submission Date: January 15, 2024

COVER LETTER

Dear FDA Review Team,

Global Pharma Inc. is pleased to submit this Investigational New Drug (IND) Application for OncoMab, a novel anti-PD-L1 monoclonal antibody for the treatment of advanced solid tumors.

1.0 DRUG SUBSTANCE

1.1 General Information
- Chemical Name: Anti-PD-L1 Humanized Monoclonal Antibody
- Molecular Weight: ~150 kDa
- Drug Substance Manufacturer: BioPharma Manufacturing LLC

1.2 Manufacturing Information
Drug substance is manufactured using CHO cell expression system following current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP).

2.0 DRUG PRODUCT

2.1 Formulation
OncoMab injection, 100 mg/10 mL single-use vial
- Active ingredient: OncoMab 10 mg/mL
- Excipients: Histidine, sucrose, polysorbate 80, water for injection

2.2 Stability
Shelf life: 24 months when stored at 2-8°C. Do not freeze.

3.0 NONCLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY AND TOXICOLOGY

3.1 Pharmacodynamics
OncoMab binds to PD-L1 with high affinity (KD = 0.28 nM) and blocks PD-1/PD-L1 interaction.

3.2 Pharmacokinetics
Single and repeat-dose toxicity studies conducted in cynomolgus monkeys demonstrated acceptable safety profile.

3.3 Safety Pharmacology
- Cardiovascular: No effects on heart rate, blood pressure, or ECG parameters
- Respiratory: No effects on respiratory rate or tidal volume
- CNS: No behavioral or neurological effects observed

4.0 CLINICAL PROTOCOL

4.1 Phase I Dose Escalation Study
Title: A Phase I Dose Escalation Study of OncoMab in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors

4.2 Study Objectives
Primary: Determine maximum tolerated dose (MTD) and recommended Phase II dose (RP2D)
Secondary: Assess safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics, and preliminary efficacy

4.3 Study Design
- 3+3 dose escalation design
- Dose levels: 1, 3, 10, 20 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks
- Maximum 6 dose levels planned

5.0 INVESTIGATOR INFORMATION

Principal Investigator: Dr. Sarah Johnson, MD, PhD
Institution: Cancer Research Institute
Qualifications: Board-certified oncologist with 15 years experience in early-phase oncology trials

6.0 REGULATORY HISTORY

6.1 FDA Interactions
- Pre-IND meeting held December 10, 2023

6.2 International Regulatory Status
- European CTA submitted to MHRA (pending approval)

7.0 RISK ASSESSMENT

7.1 Identified Risks
- Immune-related adverse events (based on drug class)
- Infusion-related reactions
- Potential for autoimmune toxicity

7.2 Risk Mitigation
- Comprehensive safety monitoring plan
- Pre-medication protocol for infusion reactions
- Immunosuppressive therapy algorithms

8.0 BENEFIT-RISK ASSESSMENT

The nonclinical data support the safety of OncoMab for human testing. The proposed Phase I study design includes appropriate safety measures and dose escalation strategy.
