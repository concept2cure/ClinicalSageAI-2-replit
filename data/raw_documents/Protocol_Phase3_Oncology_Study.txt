Clinical Protocol: Phase III Oncology Study
Protocol ID: ONC-P3-2025-001
Version: 3.0
Effective Date: 2025-06-01

1. Introduction
This protocol details the conduct of a pivotal Phase III study for investigational drug 'OncoMab'. The study targets advanced solid tumors and represents a critical milestone in the development program.

2. Objectives
2.1 Primary Objective: To evaluate efficacy and safety of OncoMab compared to standard of care in patients with advanced solid tumors.
2.2 Secondary Objective: Pharmacokinetics assessment and biomarker analysis.
2.3 Exploratory Objective: Quality of life assessment using EORTC QLQ-C30.

3. Study Design
This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study with a planned enrollment of 450 patients across 25 international sites.

3.1 Study Population
Adults (≥18 years) with histologically confirmed advanced solid tumors who have failed at least one prior systemic therapy.

3.2 Randomization
Patients will be randomized 2:1 to receive either OncoMab or placebo.

4. Treatment Plan
4.1 Investigational Product
OncoMab 10mg/kg IV every 3 weeks for up to 12 cycles or until disease progression.

4.2 Concomitant Medications
Standard supportive care is permitted per institutional guidelines.

5. Adverse Events
All adverse events must be reported to the IRB within 24 hours. Serious Adverse Events (SAEs) require immediate notification to FDA and EMA within 15 calendar days.

5.1 Safety Monitoring
- Monthly Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) reviews
- Interim safety analysis after 150 patients
- Pre-specified stopping rules for efficacy and futility

6. Patient Population
Adults with confirmed diagnosis of advanced solid tumors, ECOG performance status 0-2, adequate organ function.

7. Statistical Considerations
7.1 Primary Endpoint: Overall Survival (OS)
7.2 Secondary Endpoints: Progression-Free Survival (PFS), Objective Response Rate (ORR)
7.3 Sample Size: 450 patients provide 90% power to detect HR=0.75

8. Regulatory Considerations
This study is conducted under IND 123456 and EudraCT 2025-001234-56.
