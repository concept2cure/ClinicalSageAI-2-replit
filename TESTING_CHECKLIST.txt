## Common Questions & Answers

### Q: Where do I start?
**A**: Read BROWSER_TESTING_GUIDE.md first, then follow along in your browser.

### Q: What if I see errors?
**A**: Check browser console (F12 → Console). Common errors are listed in BROWSER_TESTING_GUIDE.md.

### Q: How long will testing take?
**A**: 15-30 minutes for full browser testing following the guide.

### Q: What if features don't work?
**A**: Check TESTING_REPORT.md troubleshooting section and browser console for clues.

### Q: Can I test offline?
**A**: Yes, but export/import features require you to be online for some operations.

### Q: How do I report an issue?
**A**: Include: what you did, what happened, what you expected, console error (if any), screenshot.
